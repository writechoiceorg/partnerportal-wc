*Please ensure that Opportunities is fully set up. To learn more, check out this [article](https://help.partnerportal.io/en/articles/9091768-how-to-set-up-opportunities).*

Once you've set up Opportunities, here's how to effectively use them within PartnerPortal.io:

# Creating a New Opportunity:

- Go to the **Opportunities** tab in the sidebar menu.

- Click on the **Add New** button.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2386705731/935881f405c97ab6aae594aa02d3/image.png?expires=1779112800&amp;signature=74736774cc8586139ff65a1fe1fc088d6a2cf82bcc3cd32871f77dc009d50caa&amp;req=diMvEM5%2BmIZcWPMW1HO4zfHNYX9nm1S9Kj353v4j9FYG%2BUKM8OnM7t8QRoaU%0At145fyeZTzpHiVgKT3Q%3D%0A)

- Fill in all necessary information for the opportunity, including contact information, budget, service type, and any additional details.

- Click **Save** to create the opportunity.

- On the Partners section, click **Manage** to open the partner selection window. Select the partner(s) you want to send the opportunity to from the available partner list.

  ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2386712325/99fe5c1bd34622418ff38577a668/image.png?expires=1779112800&amp;signature=c414193062664fec25321d3099c85fd62bc7b635a68211b40e090d6a4a5e0b7c&amp;req=diMvEM5%2Fn4JdXPMW1HO4zX1Li6mAdWCaO3TUSz7VD3BAo8gsNJdaHbHf5nNa%0Al9Nj%0A)
  ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2386713673/75305acc9c0ac50399f2e676f789/image.png?expires=1779112800&amp;signature=a21d1e9567153dbbec551d07136d0357bf0376c8cb11eda7f80d4d80d4708133&amp;req=diMvEM5%2FnodYWvMW1HO4zQYGqZWVhBlYag%2FoFu%2Bhff%2FZMJBKg4324cl%2F7%2F90%0ANe%2FY%0A)

- Click **Save** to send the opportunity to the selected partners.

# Managing Opportunities:

- Monitor the status of sent opportunities in the "Opportunities" tab (updated by partners).

- Use the comment section to communicate with partners regarding specific opportunities.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2386719319/1ae51674b06eee194e4f78e9e083/image.png?expires=1779112800&amp;signature=0f3e6ffd7d0079bfcd1ae8223a242b87c220558ff862537023ccf6bf66a0dd56&amp;req=diMvEM5%2FlIJeUPMW1HO4zYDH9l0rMQwhRPVzxBNu%2B9yXne7meblONZSk0BPA%0ASM8CunTRZBmPxlqU0lM%3D%0A)

- Partners will receive notifications and can log in to the portal to review and respond to opportunities.

# Removing Opportunities:

**Archiving:** If you are no longer working on an opportunity with a partner, you can archive it. This will make the opportunity inactive and stop all communications, but the historical data will still be available to you.

- Click on the respective opportunity.

- Click the Archive button and confirm the action in the pop-up.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2386721600/bf111d6feafc583fdc07cf2dcfdd/image.png?expires=1779112800&amp;signature=1daf672444d7dc49ce3e52397ee609a5ed4891cfc4ad07f5b4c79f36353f61bb&amp;req=diMvEM58nIdfWfMW1HO4zfHPo%2BMTYRykyJ%2B1lZaaR0mYXJsO20S9Q%2FxhfdOo%0AK8LmXaObJ4AIcHIxoD8%3D%0A)

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2386722013/dbec051a6c29c87a36ea15f93c97/image.png?expires=1779112800&amp;signature=0738ced054f745d22b2f9cd3cafff0c3de6e754845775e6522be1fa661613e6d&amp;req=diMvEM58n4FeWvMW1HO4zegucRMNvrJU0M5HcX9Gx%2BrdiT4XheU3xgERfa6u%0Adcd7YATdP9P%2Bb98%2BPlk%3D%0A)

​**Deleting:** If you want to completely delete an opportunity, it must first be archived.

- Open the archived opportunity.

- Click the **Delete Opportunity** button.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2386722957/257b2d5091023fe4852423a0bb79/image.png?expires=1779112800&amp;signature=c01d76aeb0ee2643d003831a068692670d0d5dcd73b59a6a44a3c5f8217d4976&amp;req=diMvEM58n4haXvMW1HO4zdQQptJIXfF%2FjVI2Qhv2nTz04H0778lvd%2BnuJI34%0ATL5TFWKF70PklbZofsE%3D%0A)

- Confirm the action in the pop-up.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2386723858/7b96b5c22237dc6a38237317f174/image.png?expires=1779112800&amp;signature=7881fd842d5316844cd516bee630db53457fc35d87bd07489179f1d7133c1551&amp;req=diMvEM58nolaUfMW1HO4zaXPNO3TuHVx3q6BZwaDsPIlwxWIDmErBzrMfMXB%0AIV5pKVlXog%2F9T29pYLg%3D%0A)
