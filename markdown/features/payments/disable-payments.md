---
title: "How to Disable Payments"
description: "This article explains how to remove payment-related features and references from the portal."
---

Not all partner programs include commission payouts. If your program does not use payments, you can disable them so they no longer appear in the partner portal.

---

## Disable Payments

1. Go to **Portal Settings** → **Payment Settings**.

2. Find the **Disable Payments Interface** section.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2386696215/712a8ae0014764d56b13630e20d9/image.png?expires=1779112800&amp;signature=8da25e76eb31133cac20ba0438990b06f778763e0e4804f813ff7494bcabf16c&amp;req=diMvEM93m4NeXPMW1HO4zTuYNtgk2GCp46BlMRLHChY%2FAgIsLAWYJpcv%2BcIB%0AtHp4EkPd6ol7s7Ws2Lk%3D%0A)

Before you can toggle Payments off, the following features must be disabled:

- **Partner Tiers**

- **Commission Generation**

- **Accounting Integration**

- **Payments Integration**

Once these features are disabled, you can toggle **Payments** off and payment references will be hidden throughout the portal.
