# Partner Tiers

Partner Tiers allow you to reward partners with different commission structures based on revenue performance over a set period.

Instead of assigning a single commission structure to all partners, you can define revenue thresholds that unlock higher commission levels.

---

## Commission Structures in Partner Tiers

Partner Tiers can apply the same commission structures supported throughout the platform:

- **Fixed amount**

- **Recurring percentage of Revenue**

- **One-time percentage of Revenue**

This allows you to design a program that scales as your partners generate more revenue.

---

# Setting Up Your Tiers

To get started:

- Scroll down to find your Partner Tiers section and click on the Pencil to edit.

## 1.Define Your Base Tier

Your base tier is the default commission structure partners receive before reaching any revenue thresholds.

You can define:

- The revenue requirement

- The commission type (one-time percentage, fixed, recurring)

This becomes the starting point for your program where **all** partners will start.

---

## 2.Add Revenue Thresholds

You can add as many tiers as your program requires.

For each tier, define:

- Tier Name

- Revenue threshold

- Commission type

- Optional color coding

When a partner reaches a new tier, they will see their updated tier (including color) when logged into their portal.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2361987402/ba294ebe8de6b3591fdd4ab956aa/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=4303f7b8fd1ce7ce3a3fb60b4ae414cfc4ee9948841e297d8b2406af3a6ebc82&amp;req=diMhF8B2moVfW%2FMW1HO4zdpqQkZ2aOi1SrRDegR2kFWcUfigprV3q3XM7l8v%0ACWsUW%2BFO8EWNQNbeC6I%3D%0A)

If Partner Tiers are marked 'Public' they will be visible in the Program tab in your sign-in page.

---

## Tier Calculation Frequency

Choose how often tiers should be evaluated:

- **Monthly**

- **Quarterly**

- **Annually**

The system reviews all eligible revenue from Leads submitted during the selected period. Based on **total revenue** within that timeframe, the partner is assigned the appropriate tier.

Commissions for that period are calculated according to the tier achieved.

---

## Final Step

After configuring your tiers and frequency, click **Save** to apply your Partner Tier structure.
