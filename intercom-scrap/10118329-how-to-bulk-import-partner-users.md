# As An Admin

- Navigate to your **All** **Partners** section under **Partners** on the side menu and locate the partner for whom you'd like to import users.

- Scroll to the bottom of the page and find the **Partner Users** section.

- Click **Add Partner User** and select **Import CSV**.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388498552/2402fa8ffe98ae597338ba0f0839/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=7db09d7cf0b1b1b96ff32caf6988e860b94a557ae5d4cbfcc13403b894e5faa5&amp;req=diMvHs13lYRaW%2FMW1HO4zXI2NEzYgtxWiKVnIV6HQ%2BPQYu0af%2FvjZt%2FSOLMZ%0A5fBzBnlnQmtZLuLGq%2B0%3D%0A)

- In the new window, download the template if you haven’t already. Once ready, click on **Select CSV file**, choose your file, and then click **Import Partner Users**.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388499954/75a6fa54a31896538c14692ce392/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=b7fcc1864766a6ca47ea5addf71b0bdb7e8adb3a521d4ce4e3f48446c6de4718&amp;req=diMvHs13lIhaXfMW1HO4zYEzT4ARco0hldkwPU1YiLKE%2F7x3%2Fq6AsW%2Fyu9CE%0AOUhrcSqMb3cJzll6o7I%3D%0A)

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388502205/e43de8590c629e95cb3b87a49896/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=a9d7501b6adbc77044c264e04e14e78c8dfa62a1fa057b3a863506b7acf34a73&amp;req=diMvHsx%2Bn4NfXPMW1HO4zUf%2F5MmQ%2FuJDnAvRT5YnPqlaq8bYcGGJ2JOLYKAM%0AGhdHtjWfMVvaUL4ZysE%3D%0A)

- If there are errors in the CSV file, an error message will indicate what needs fixing. Correct the errors and try uploading your CSV again.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388507956/e7a2ba83fc08910b3fa835309873/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=d529a0a249351553a3acc682ff126ba4ef71ffc8673070a16191d1d5e86956ea&amp;req=diMvHsx%2BmohaX%2FMW1HO4zR6J%2FA5Uzfcwh6vtinJwZHVqR7zG785VQzjZ0bVm%0AFpeu%2FNBtBTmEXbRwGsc%3D%0A)

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388555614/674436ad514774a74ca52673b720/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=4430a5e0b78b50535903a72b9023dc1533be4ee96dc58d697f5b7f74195d3a9f&amp;req=diMvHsx7mIdeXfMW1HO4zXYHlt1y9kl79sQR6%2BHUEHoaiMBpYyFE3naHnm7g%0ACaWqjYTGJQBxns9Y2DA%3D%0A)

- Once the import is successful, all users will receive an email with instructions to set up their account.

*For a visual walkthrough, check out our [step-by-step video](https://youtu.be/tIDcxbJurr0) guide.*

If the link in their welcome email expires, they can request a password reset with [these steps](https://help.partnerportal.io/en/articles/8694347-how-to-reset-password) to complete the setup.

# As A Partner

As a **Partner Owner**, you can also add your team members in bulk using this feature.

- Go to the **Team** tab under **Account**.

- Click **Add Team Member** and select **Import CSV**.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388570476/6b49a41a59eea15e3d1deebbc0bd/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=e52b9c5288a9701edd9bb9c8d39a829d53166fdf784f2801b0e164ca7094c38f&amp;req=diMvHsx5nYVYX%2FMW1HO4zdpT8%2B0%2FVXeVrIJyM2tu924YRlkdzl5bISWRmANn%0AzcyUzJhLER%2BqywwlkUk%3D%0A)

- Download the template if you haven't already. When ready, select your CSV file.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388573253/9a0cc7c75505b350003cd33cd31f/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=3902ec4e1b1f5398ce8e669350bb08df3c9df676a11c668e7d000c2cc515830e&amp;req=diMvHsx5noNaWvMW1HO4zVahjkm9U%2B5jVLD4PNjJbZ6K80zLdleD2c8ypdw7%0Ak9D8ZfO9TpSEJ9YKgQ8%3D%0A)

- Import your team members.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388575974/14a9b5ab82a2cfaafde42848269c/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=8d14bafa9e5976c33f29e0d963ad9c78b1de7e86f96878da330e801524db4490&amp;req=diMvHsx5mIhYXfMW1HO4zVvHkwjoTC%2BvizRdN29MZsO55cQlmwFid4MTB6TH%0AKgsMuI%2BIGCHeHCQI8%2Fo%3D%0A)

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388576622/cfd857239a7282dfd70dea5d8881/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=20481d340eb857c8713bbea074f629d09672da2450c4d82cab773219a35a7a8d&amp;req=diMvHsx5m4ddW%2FMW1HO4zSwTW5FiaTwrUHZ0%2ByalLFfCB4lGpE%2FxqnHw1%2BIi%0AKfVWxDqbomew0T1BfNk%3D%0A)

- If there are errors in the CSV, you’ll see a message detailing what needs fixing. Correct any issues and re-upload the file.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388577587/9dee7507fd3abd44050181f9ea54/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=162480b53a20bed7168340cb887e49be6fce2875a0ce29eb2bcb905188554600&amp;req=diMvHsx5moRXXvMW1HO4zU9yeruIpdsjQ6yUb7s9c6krJf5kM%2FsEMcnFNY6a%0A92ibFiV88J30pIPZ%2FsE%3D%0A)

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388579410/5b70b41cbe03f8de174119c2ee75/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=7392f453c7e99bad00b96469d5e2221f969ff8bfb5bc6f110b6d28a6ec3e41cb&amp;req=diMvHsx5lIVeWfMW1HO4zSWbzkoIh%2Bay5xUmxbEWW6dcCTaZPDVtfcfu8Nic%0AvNnEyUpbAL1MBJmkx2A%3D%0A)

- Once the import is successful, all users will receive an email with instructions to set up their account.

If the link in their welcome email expires, they can request a password reset with [these steps](https://help.partnerportal.io/en/articles/8694347-how-to-reset-password) to complete the setup.
