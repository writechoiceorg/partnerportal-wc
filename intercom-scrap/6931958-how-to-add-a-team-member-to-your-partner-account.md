A Team Member can be anyone within your organization who needs access to the partner portal, such as an account executive or other internal user.

**Note:** Only Partner Owners and Partner Admins can invite new team members.

### Before You Add a Team Member

Each team member receives their own unique login and can only access the portal after being invited.

---

# Adding a Team Member

1. Select **Team** under **Portal Settings** from the sidebar.

2. Click **Add Team Member** in the upper-right corner.

  ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388467511/553f7b86b6918988a0607576b116/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=a897b95766cf0a57ccf11e1077553707f3597e66d787e24557db0a57e9456a20&amp;req=diMvHs14moReWPMW1HO4zcDyUgUaZC0ch5XILrpGLK6Uis33pRBkCdRgDeED%0AKHEH%0A)

3. Enter the following required information:

- First Name

- Last Name

- Email Address

- Phone Number (11 digits, including country code)

**All** fields are required to send the invitation.

If you do not have an active phone number, any 11-digit number can be used.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388468280/4200f8e6f1a951f677557eecdb7a/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=fd320bb474418e8b73cd362a5ffc19ea85a1b867f0a98dbef69e4bc252586d85&amp;req=diMvHs14lYNXWfMW1HO4zVyxMBkosUuVfMOiIbQqYY%2BByO6J7ixa4EMbZ4YM%0ALpENx27Z2Aj7GZUdfoU%3D%0A)

Once the user is added, you can adjust their role and permissions to determine their visibility.

*For more information on the different team member visibility, check out the following [article](https://help.partnerportal.io/en/articles/6931934-what-visibility-do-team-members-have).*

# Completing Account Registration

After the invitation is sent, the team member will receive an email to join your organization.

They must follow the link in the email to create a password and complete their account setup.

**Important:** The invitation link expires after a limited time. If it expires before registration is completed, the team member will need to request a [password reset link](https://help.partnerportal.io/en/articles/8694347-how-to-reset-password).
