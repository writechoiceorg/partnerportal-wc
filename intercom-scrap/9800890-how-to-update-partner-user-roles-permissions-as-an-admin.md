As a Portal Admin or Portal Owner, you can update the roles & permissions for Partner Users (partner's team). Click on the **All Partners** tab under **Partners** in the sidebar and select the Partner Company.

Scroll down to the Partner Users section. Select the Edit button next to their name.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388486403/b6605fb195370802eb63c9eba532/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=cf9ac05fea0923346384e60cc54861a90cf376a36ac52b8ab6555c222125fd3d&amp;req=diMvHs12m4VfWvMW1HO4zaHvaM%2FMaRo%2BxNP2ITzfO3lCA%2B1nIkvFfe7X7Dnu%0A46IFpyV4pHAPiCLDHgc%3D%0A)

Once selected, a pop-up window will appear and show you the different permissions available. Select the desired one and press save.
