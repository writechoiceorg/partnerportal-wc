Customizing your Lead Registration Form is simple with the portal's flexible form feature. You can tailor the form by adding or editing fields and questions that partners will fill out when submitting leads. This makes it easy for partners to submit leads on your behalf.

# Adding A New Field

To begin, go to the **Configure** tab under **Leads**.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388636749/16c8ffcd3217da9deb03a7e38a95/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=0d8c455d9c042234d236a0bfc22e5a6281c9635d289d65ffc0ed662be3671401&amp;req=diMvHs99m4ZbUPMW1HO4zUjC%2Bx2W3dDDUzzBAH%2B3uJJSu60pK2XfVoeoW4bv%0AP8bq%2BX%2B3NTT3EHV9YcI%3D%0A)

*If you need more information on how to Submit Leads on Behalf of Partners, check out this [article](https://help.partnerportal.io/en/articles/7045874-submit-a-lead-on-behalf-of-a-partner).*

You can add different field types such as **Text**, **Numbers**, **Picklist**, or **Large Text** and rearrange them in any order. To add a new field, click the plus sign at the bottom of the lead fields list.

Note: Some fields are not customizable, as they are essential for CRM integration (e.g., Lead First Name, Last Name, Email). However, you can deactivate some of these pre-set fields if preferred.

Make sure to check the **Active** box next to each field to include it on the Lead Form. If you mark a field as **Required**, partners must complete it before submitting a lead.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388640263/15883a2bd7102857e5e7161990b0/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=a6594f832f14c026714aa7b1e89730684022827c1cbc4fc65c43174cbfd7400b&amp;req=diMvHs96nYNZWvMW1HO4zeKql1B%2B1CUmQWGynsmPAIVC4hfG5Sq8DVa9qIWD%0AEhdPImKzy%2BwNrSOlsrI%3D%0A)

# Setting Visibility

You'll have the option to choose what visibility your lead fields should have by setting the visibility. In the visibility section, select the drop-down to begin customizing.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388642772/cc670b4f9dbc88157efa275a5faf/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=d1fe76a9dae2b39aa42a555b146414176edc622b990a955f101c503b0a770414&amp;req=diMvHs96n4ZYW%2FMW1HO4zdpDTN7MGJGPmznSaFVRWYZKoLQoXbbVwq4mkw2A%0AausntSU4UFTcjEESPSQ%3D%0A)

The options are*:*

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388644968/149dd852c813d3e49b07c30859ae/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=6bfdb44b7ce23c4c9257a4e406a12f7a2969d142f16ca812c64f3e4083d8ee22&amp;req=diMvHs96mYhZUfMW1HO4zQZWlmehv7PfTkRYkW%2BwmD%2FPMHfjVyDvemRW7UhJ%0A6%2F3XvfnO9fMKHXLLzSA%3D%0A)

# How to create a Picklist?

To create a picklist, go to the **Type** section and select **Picklist** from the dropdown. Click **Edit Options** to add your picklist choices by clicking the plus sign. After adding your options, click **Close**. Remember to save your changes regularly to avoid losing any progress.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388659984/6c2225f52ef5204c914ee2d8973d/2026-05-15_10-40-20+-1-.gif?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=6f668ed335f002bd91c73ad39d5827c5b364560db09122122121c20d4e569a4d&amp;req=diMvHs97lIhXXfMW1HO4zRJbe%2Bfc75HISGlf6MEw43yjtTPicVnNtO7zWDul%0A7ntKerc6MqLIoQTa9uA%3D%0A)

# Creating your Lead Type Drop-down

On the Lead Registration Form, you'll find a drop-down that is called Type. This drop-down field can be configured to match your use case. Common examples of lead types are:

- Type of lead (e.g., "New Business" vs. "Existing Client")

- Type of partner

- Location of the partner

- Type of product

Select the Edit Options button to begin customizing your desired lead types.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388664763/62ed07b3c24a1af61676d07ffc45/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=cb252ebee648ad3925d35c7ce597eed3d571b179676b370d37088c01de290416&amp;req=diMvHs94mYZZWvMW1HO4zXoMdgUQMIxMIkcD%2B%2F4sHeZNOvQ5QFRztgBmvpoD%0A%2BVD2Tau4NRQHvTfNc5M%3D%0A)

Note, that your partners can only select 1 lead type when submitting leads, but if you'd like them to be able to enter a different lead type, toggle on the "Other option".

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388666062/4300c650a9c7e1d9436ad1e17453/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=6c788a018f047f538b81d1a0400bb6d575599808e215ce22291215e9001f948d&amp;req=diMvHs94m4FZW%2FMW1HO4zWu%2FA1yJnfTRCKz6K0UwsDpxx1Qhf19wOL1Jgdxa%0AVw9mURy7PWf%2B7vGQ0R4%3D%0A)
