*Please ensure that Opportunities is fully set up. To learn more, check out this [article](https://help.partnerportal.io/en/articles/9091768-how-to-set-up-opportunities).*

Once you've set up Opportunities, here's how to effectively use them within PartnerPortal.io:

# Creating a New Opportunity:

- Go to the **Opportunities** tab in the sidebar menu.

- Click on the **Add New** button.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2386705731/935881f405c97ab6aae594aa02d3/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=77b21c31e4e14326b355e876005de938b00eadcdf27bf3ac1f3efa0e588c193b&amp;req=diMvEM5%2BmIZcWPMW1HO4zfHNbnVmm1C0Kj353v4j9FYkCTivNrKoi2iLUv19%0ATKcD%2BEQgjEOoF%2BPMeqs%3D%0A)

- Fill in all necessary information for the opportunity, including contact information, budget, service type, and any additional details.

- Click **Save** to create the opportunity.

- On the Partners section, click **Manage** to open the partner selection window. Select the partner(s) you want to send the opportunity to from the available partner list.

  ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2386712325/99fe5c1bd34622418ff38577a668/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=56548775252e91c501db3815e0135353dc9b140e823ec9a92e15d9997ec602d9&amp;req=diMvEM5%2Fn4JdXPMW1HO4zX1LhKOBdWSTO3TUSz7VD3AFMlP8PB8cmqrBH3DA%0ArCm4%0A)
  ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2386713673/75305acc9c0ac50399f2e676f789/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=668c714e14bfb387e699d1b1b6376534d345bde1c90950ff7b102adfde062c95&amp;req=diMvEM5%2FnodYWvMW1HO4zQYGpp%2BUhB1Rag%2FoFu%2Bhff%2BqjbFc6YinYxfM3oSZ%0AYOZh%0A)

- Click **Save** to send the opportunity to the selected partners.

# Managing Opportunities:

- Monitor the status of sent opportunities in the "Opportunities" tab (updated by partners).

- Use the comment section to communicate with partners regarding specific opportunities.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2386719319/1ae51674b06eee194e4f78e9e083/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=6b27dad50b1e263dc55ec5195662c8f4f33e357ec40938837ca733995ebfc84d&amp;req=diMvEM5%2FlIJeUPMW1HO4zYDH%2BVcqMQgoRPVzxBNu%2B9yvK768U8oDrrLkgSHz%0AO7hBe%2FwklmMx%2BsFP5oE%3D%0A)

- Partners will receive notifications and can log in to the portal to review and respond to opportunities.

# Removing Opportunities:

**Archiving:** If you are no longer working on an opportunity with a partner, you can archive it. This will make the opportunity inactive and stop all communications, but the historical data will still be available to you.

- Click on the respective opportunity.

- Click the Archive button and confirm the action in the pop-up.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2386721600/bf111d6feafc583fdc07cf2dcfdd/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=0a4c9623f8679b54685f4f1d99649d3f6513199d5df66c04f0fcb95272f4e962&amp;req=diMvEM58nIdfWfMW1HO4zfHPrOkSYRityJ%2B1lZaaR0n9dnGumClKvDOqfV3z%0Av0Cy39vt1mv6oSFeWAI%3D%0A)

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2386722013/dbec051a6c29c87a36ea15f93c97/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=9da4cc413bfffdd4834a78b048f9088590725d1e84b83793e1c0b41ce78f9472&amp;req=diMvEM58n4FeWvMW1HO4zegufhkMvrZd0M5HcX9Gx%2BoaI3IbcOMsWwQ6DIQb%0AgJZ7jUZbAG5BVwt0rGc%3D%0A)

​**Deleting:** If you want to completely delete an opportunity, it must first be archived.

- Open the archived opportunity.

- Click the **Delete Opportunity** button.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2386722957/257b2d5091023fe4852423a0bb79/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=489ffd803252210a9df403af92a707f0a7490d2277963fa0acbdcc5c376ca7d1&amp;req=diMvEM58n4haXvMW1HO4zdQQqdhJXfV2jVI2Qhv2nTx4FE5xOxx4glFnCBMK%0Aks8G534TBtoPm7L3vdo%3D%0A)

- Confirm the action in the pop-up.
