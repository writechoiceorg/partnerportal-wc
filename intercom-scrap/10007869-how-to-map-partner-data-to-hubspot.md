You may want to map Partner information from the portal to your HubSpot deal.

In the **Integrations** tab, open your Hubspot configuration.

In the **custom field add-on section** (bottom of the Deal object), click the dropdown to view all the custom fields in your pipeline. Select the field you want to map. (If you don't have a field for Partner information yet, [see this[HubSpot article](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/properties/create-and-edit-properties) on adding properties].)

After selecting the custom field, press **Add** and click on the newly added field.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2377298214/5774d2e952fd583a3652ef6b02ea/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=67fa8ecd8169be791f8082c081897d82c71e71dbf414581f46d9499da62d7695&amp;req=diMgEct3lYNeXfMW1HO4zYpENsFqbB45qUByIB0%2BLJ8mlqALJ%2Fvkzf4md4w6%0A8f5cuPjaci1jnsco1v8%3D%0A)

Set the **Type** to **Portal object** and choose the **Object** (Partner or Partner User). In the **Field** dropdown, select the portal field you want mapped.

- **Partner**: Maps Partner Company details only.

- **Partner User**: Options to map pp.io ID, First/Last Name, Email, or Phone number.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2377298893/a0069e3fe2c8301abc2eb72e27a6/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=ac1c0aca1f107f65928e2b34f2ea99beb6df6e68bb79f75fb0a9552a050e5d20&amp;req=diMgEct3lYlWWvMW1HO4zas8VJdDfKeX0QyE%2BvD5RueWZddD0tujKFKs6cGk%0Ap5uqRW0HRrSO45zk45k%3D%0A)

Once selected, Partner information will automatically map to your HubSpot deal when a deal is created.

To learn more about creating custom fields, [check out this [article](https://help.partnerportal.io/en/articles/6977660-how-to-map-custom-fields-to-hubspot)].
