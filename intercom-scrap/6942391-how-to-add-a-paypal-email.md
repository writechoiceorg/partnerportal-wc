If you will be receiving PayPal payments please ensure you have a PayPal account. Once your account is created, and you've connected it to your bank account or any other account, you'll need to enter that email in the portal.

## **How to add a PayPal email as a Partner?**

Log into your partner account and head to your *Account & Settings* tab and select *My Account*. Click on the pencil icon in the top right to edit, and enter your PayPal email in the designated field. Remember to press Save!

## How to add a PayPal Email for a Partner?

If you'd like to add a PayPal account email on behalf of your partner you'll want to first click on the *Partners* tab in your portal > Select the Partners name > click on the *Details* button > click the pencil icon to edit > enter PayPal email in the Payment information section.
