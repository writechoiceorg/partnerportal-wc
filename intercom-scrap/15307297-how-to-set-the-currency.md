This article will walk you through the steps on how to set the currency that will be accepted in your portal.

After your first payment, the currency is locked and it's not possible to change it again.

## Set the currency

1. Log in to your portal.

2. Navigate to ***Payment Settings*** under ***Portal Settings***.

3. At ***General Payment Settings*** click on the pencil icon to Edit.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2439207610/f327b192eb7408645a54569cbcf7/8ff8a2d8-f25c-4dd9-a547-e23acc79a31d?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=bf15e20c4b54d048f902a0c2b16a9457ed3f68b10509877f616a0c192e9bc821&amp;req=diQkH8t%2BmodeWfMW1HO4zUAhZXpz%2FgdMouvGQOAcYW3PbER2hWfWDGxhCyWN%0AjbQ0u8sU3lH71rDBxB8%3D%0A)

1. On the **Currency** option click on the drop-down menu and choose the currency.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2439207612/7bb189753572cf6c259ecb4bfaa5/ad5a005b-2b56-4022-a1cb-1493ab403d4c?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=5e396d1311763eb64f7060a7c06ad87d8e1b0536a9ab699cd9ecace3cba28ef8&amp;req=diQkH8t%2BmodeW%2FMW1HO4zQvJ0cr2t4aDKTS8VDkRG27Vovlb7C0sHMVHF6nN%0APFEVAfpKvHeddcb82Ro%3D%0A)

1. Click Save at the end of the ***General Payment Settings*** section.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2439207611/c6df131fd3a1ae97c5ac154df5cc/1d135644-cd35-455a-b22c-d50e64c80edd?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=2fe60771c8fb0b4ac57ba766d7bfce87c2d0a82fa46e4cfc3a8b13ca390c5842&amp;req=diQkH8t%2BmodeWPMW1HO4zUmw72ubbW%2Bx3OJSI5VzncCKYgLVXGOq4zhdSpzh%0Auub8qJJus%2F46RSaKtTA%3D%0A)

Once completed the currency accepted at the portal will be set. Remember that the currency set is **locked after the first payment**.
