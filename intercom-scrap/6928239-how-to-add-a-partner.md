# Option 1: Partner Self-Registration

If you'd like partners to sign up on their own, they can register directly through your portal’s registration page.

## Step 1: Share the Registration Link

Send your partners the registration page so they can create their own account.

Your registration page will follow this format:

`yourcompanyname.partnerportal.io/register`

You can include this link in an email or other communication introducing your partner portal and explaining how they can join the program.

This step can only be done by 1 user at each partner company. Once the registered user has access to the portal, they can invite the remainder of their team.

To avoid duplicate partner companie, do not send these steps to multiple users at a single company.

## Step 2: Partner Registration

Once a partner lands on the registration page, they can complete the sign-up process to request access to your partner program.

**All** fields in the registration form will need to be completed by the registering partner.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388394489/263c25fd269fde70bc27b8bc7283/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=7e47617ab9542d1e3bdc1ebd4cbd60596fdbc2c9aa5010c4cdce404fd87a4171&amp;req=diMvHsp3mYVXUPMW1HO4zXy39wFMsJrzVp20Ln3AZydlcyBTjRYPt5TIGt9g%0ARjhBK5i%2BantNSlKx57U%3D%0A)

## Step 3: Approve the Partner

After a partner submits their registration request:

- The partner will receive an email confirming their request was submitted.

- The portal **Admin team will be notified** of the request.

- Once the Admin verifies and approves the partner, they will gain access to the portal and can begin registering deals.

**Important:**
PartnerPortal does **not support automatic partner approval**. All partner registrations must be manually approved by an Admin.

# Option 2: Invite a Partner

You can also invite partners directly to the portal without requiring them to complete the registration form.

## Step 1: Send an Invite

1. Go to the **Invite** **Partner** under the **Partners** menu section.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2361889247/c8f58494dcacba9437a1926f126d/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=540637b6f6703c62522b495408062b7359f554a2adb5bb012170605bdc415a56&amp;req=diMhF8F2lINbXvMW1HO4ze3j1SB7GUebnwS2gEasmNOd1a13ey36sOn944mQ%0A6jbmsc8NXiRKT4l8AvA%3D%0A)

From here, you can invite **a single partner** or **multiple partners at once**.

#### **Important:** All **fields in the invite form must be completed.**

Phone Number Format Requirements:

- Enter the **full number without spaces or dashes**

- Include the **country code**

Example:
​`14075557899`
(1 = US country code + 4075557899)

## Bulk Inviting Partners

To invite multiple partners at once, you can upload a **CSV file**.

1. Download the **CSV template** in the bulk invite section.

2. Add your partner information to the template.

3. Upload the completed file.

The bulk invite page will also show **how many partners can be invited at once**.
