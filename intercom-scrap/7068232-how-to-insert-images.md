# Inserting an Image

Images can be inserted into a Resource description, an Announcement, an Opportunity, or the Lead Comments.

Head to the portal in one of the areas mentioned above, and click on **Upload Image** in the **Featured Image** box.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2364689190/fa1d97f2932cc2b724bf93fd644c/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=ab8ec125855383f880aac8cb257c52613ae41270b6f719ff0cfc2d460990bc90&amp;req=diMhEs92lIBWWfMW1HO4zXfkQ5tA3%2BcyfgNxJOcwgBFt0iUOKYBjiOC27hnO%0AuqElcPbtrnsE2ffUqU0%3D%0A)

You can upload a file from your computer. Keep in mind the file size limit.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2364692706/afffcd09dbd6d949c5c7a30cf0a0/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=2780e999e5da8ffaeda6e9bcb848a780e3628133639c9ee28a7593948eca9713&amp;req=diMhEs93n4ZfX%2FMW1HO4zVsl6tpypl6UWGkCdZWbCmCQwhg%2FBGeRd6sb6CUb%0AK8N0YdWY0CwQ3kzHUWE%3D%0A)

You can also insert images within the content of the announcement, resource, opportunity or lead comment. To do so, click on **Insert** on the text editor and choose **Image**.

Head to the portal in one of the areas mentioned above, and click on **Insert** and select **Image**.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2364699480/a689de535aa3cf81888cf9b4381d/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=bba4ffd7c091e7b7d7bf3a1fdade3da2315195942e770142b09cb5fab397d361&amp;req=diMhEs93lIVXWfMW1HO4zYPOQCraWVjExXrgkkBXdlflt8itBGz6AeYxFYfr%0A3H%2Fm6LS2jj0dKhPCoLM%3D%0A)

There are two ways of inserting images in the content: you can either upload a file or insert an image from a URL.

- To Upload a file, choose the **Upload** option, then browse your files or drop an image in the box.

  ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2364706952/24848083e7eb190ea20fc8e025a3/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=2359aba37dabd36273308fa8636b90ac667b8f6f53beff8c4df42b42b6c82b9d&amp;req=diMhEs5%2Bm4haW%2FMW1HO4zYCvIc4Gror%2B1gCp8EeVjqoTKIP41M2iMZt9Hj2i%0Ay%2F0w%0A)

- If you have an image sourced on a 3rd party site such as [imgbb](<>) or [imgur.com](https://imgur.com/upload), you can select **General** and enter the URL, size the image and add a description for it.

  ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2364711371/2f563a145fae982df9aa5639e3ea/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=cb78d894de86ef1cf35d3826ccb477d6511141f87027eb670a81cefb8b28b603&amp;req=diMhEs5%2FnIJYWPMW1HO4zY1NCH92emJIE02V3HNpdZeBWqxreDsui9vjZJuk%0AePtC%0A)

Either way you choose to insert the image, click the **Save** button and you have successfully added an image to your portal. For more information on the Announcements tab, head to this [article](https://help.partnerportal.io/en/articles/6931947-announcements).
