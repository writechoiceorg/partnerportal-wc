1. **Locate the Resource**

  - Navigate to **Manage** under **Resources** on the sidebar menu.

  - Click on the category where your resource is listed.

2. **Select the Resource**

  - Find the resource you wish to delete.

  - Click on the resource to open its details.

3. **Delete the Resource**

  - Scroll down and find the **Delete** button.

    ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2386622980/161396d4ba45ccc40e71a5bb0e6d/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=5f865a0dcf6583f9a566c40b01a09197600995b79e33f518b685d5b5807c8636&amp;req=diMvEM98n4hXWfMW1HO4zVa6CYA%2B4qXKTTGWLIPTe9o81O1LeU%2F4LSOvvxkf%0Adayn%0A)

4. **Confirm Deletion**

  - A confirmation prompt will appear. Confirm that you want to delete the resource.

    - ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2386625576/9fddcb1bb2df2dd79298e6df5f3a/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=913e4265ddf98ed15554b69f7cccd1ddaca86af2bd00eed756c582fc9ecab5b3&amp;req=diMvEM98mIRYX%2FMW1HO4zU%2BH1HCy8bK%2FVTYdYia3ghjQVi6siCPXQD2MGg94%0AQ539%0A)

Once confirmed, the resource will be permanently removed from your Resource Center.

*For information on how to add a resource, check out [this article](https://help.partnerportal.io/en/articles/9015869-how-to-add-a-resource).*
