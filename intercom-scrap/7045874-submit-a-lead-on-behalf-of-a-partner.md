As a Portal Owner, you can assign leads to active partner companies directly from the Partner dropdown within your portal.

The dropdown will display all partner companies currently in **Active** status. After selecting a partner company, you can choose the specific team member who should be associated to the lead.

If a partner does not appear in the dropdown, their account is not currently active.

This typically means they:

- Have not completed registration

- Are still in **Pending** status

- Have been **Declined**

- Have been **Suspended**
