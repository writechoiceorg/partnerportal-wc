To customize the lead phases in your portal, go to **Configure** under **Leads** in your sidebar.

From there, scroll to the **Lead Phases** section and select the **pencil icon** to edit your lead phases.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388629593/4055add4fe13c742702211a5b874/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=913e3179f154168d7d7dae4698240a685e26ee0134c4283aa1925eaf130f5940&amp;req=diMvHs98lIRWWvMW1HO4zRmvgn5WCQzK2s1vyOOs0FA4cbqQwX003rqyJUgT%0Ak5I79DDkEJf0jSWzTgA%3D%0A)

You can:

- **Add new lead phases**

- **Delete unused lead phases**

- **Set a default phase for new leads**

## Deleting Lead Phases

Lead phases can be deleted if they are not currently in use.

Please note:

- A phase **cannot** be deleted if a lead is currently assigned to it

- The *default phase* **cannot** be deleted

- Some phases are **required** and must remain in place

**Closed Lost, Closed Won, and n/a** are required system phases and cannot be edited.

To delete a phase, click on the trash icon next to the field.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388630544/ff99a12879ea6ca4acc9a0fab929/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=594baad1dbe96ccb5ed5c2293524dbcaa188cd369a934f2a6e0d84153967025b&amp;req=diMvHs99nYRbXfMW1HO4zW00UBDqp2nVVjssqVi0tCmR3pELOA0YH117fHyT%0AnKoG98EvjGeME40wUZY%3D%0A)

## Setting a Default Phase

You can choose which phase new leads start in by setting a default phase. All newly submitted leads will start in this phase and will remain in the selected phase while in Pending.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388631439/cbd617fe8a37b42cc603578df9a3/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=b101903feae992377507b4f272a26626739d47b661cf55af3393e1f10e17b088&amp;req=diMvHs99nIVcUPMW1HO4zXNtY1kdmzAxW3GkNRTdYX0uQyOrnwG0CBWDYscK%0AOFCz1BBD6Z%2Bt9zNaVXA%3D%0A)

*If leads remain in Pending for an extended period, make sure your default phase reflects how you want those leads displayed.*

Any phase customization connected to your CRM will only apply once a lead has been mapped.

---

If you delete a lead phase that is currently mapped to your CRM, phase updates from the CRM will no longer apply to that deleted phase, and a CRM error will be triggered instead.

A *Closed Lost* phase can trigger your PartnerPortal.io leads to expire automatically. If you prefer to expire leads manually, make sure the **"Trigger Leads to expire"** option is turned off.
