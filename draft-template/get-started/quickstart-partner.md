# Quickstart

Get your partner program live in about 15 minutes.

This guide covers the six steps to set up your portal and onboard your first partner. Follow them in order — steps marked **Optional** can be skipped and completed later.

---

## Before You Begin

- You must be a **Portal Owner** to complete the setup steps.
- Complete **Step 1 before processing any payments** — the currency setting locks permanently after the first payment is processed.

## 1. Set Your Currency

Your program currency applies to all commission calculations and partner payouts.

> **Warning:** This setting locks permanently after your first payment is processed. Confirm your currency choice before going live or inviting partners.

> **Note:** This guide is coming soon. In the meantime, contact [support@partnerportal.io](mailto:support@partnerportal.io) if you need help with this step.

## 2. Add Your Logo and Customize Colors

Before inviting partners, make your portal reflect your brand.

You can configure:
- **Primary color** — used in the sidebar, sign-in page, and links
- **Status colors** — for lead and opportunity status indicators
- **Logo** — two versions required: one on a white background, one on your primary color
- **Favicon**

A live preview updates as you make changes. All image uploads must be in **.png format**.

[Customize your portal branding →](/portal-setup/dashboard/how-to-customize-branding)

---

## 3. Configure Program Settings and Terms

Set up how your program is presented to partners and how commissions are structured.

**Program settings** let you describe your program, define your commission structure, and set up partner tiers based on revenue performance. You can mark these details as public so prospective partners see them on your sign-in page before registering.

**Program terms** are optional. If your program requires partners to agree to terms at registration, upload them under **Partner Program → Program Terms**. Note that the same terms apply to all partners — they cannot be customized per partner.

> **Note:** If you manage agreements outside the portal (e.g. via DocuSign), you can disable the Program Terms section and attach signed documents directly to partner accounts instead.

[Set up program details and tiers →](/get-started/set-up-your-partner-program/how-to-setup-program-details)

[Add program terms →](/get-started/set-up-your-partner-program/how-to-add-program-terms)

## 4. Invite Your Team *(Optional)*

If others on your team need access to the admin side of the portal, invite them from **Account & Settings → Team**.

- Only **Portal Owners** can add team members.
- You can assign each person a role to control their level of access and visibility.

## 6. Invite Your First Partner

You're ready to bring partners on board. There are two ways to do it:

**Self-registration** — Share your portal's registration link and let partners sign up on their own. You'll receive a notification and must manually approve each request.

```
yourcompanyname.partnerportal.io/register
```

**Direct invite** — Go to the **Partners** tab, click **Invite**, and add partners by email. Bulk invites are supported via CSV upload.

> ℹ️ **Note:** Only one person per partner company should register. Once they have access, they can invite the rest of their team from inside the portal. Automatic approval is not supported — all registrations require manual Admin approval.

[Invite your first partner →](/get-started/set-up-your-partner-program/how-to-add-partner)
