# What Happens When My Billing Fails?

If a payment doesn't go through, PartnerPortal gives you a grace period to update your billing details before any access is affected.

---

## The Grace Period

When a charge fails, PartnerPortal automatically retries the payment **three more times over approximately one week**.

During this period:
- You and your partners retain **full access** to the portal.
- You will receive **email notifications** about the failed charge with instructions to update your payment method.

---

## If the Grace Period Expires

If the balance remains unpaid after the grace period ends, your portal will be **locked**.

> ⚠️ **Warning:** Once locked, neither your staff nor your partners will be able to log in to the portal or access lead data.

---

## How to Update Your Billing

Any **Portal Owner** can update the payment method by logging in to the portal. Upon login, PartnerPortal will display a prompt about the failed payment and guide you through updating your billing details.

---

## Still Having Trouble?

If you are unable to resolve the issue on your own, contact the support team at [support@partnerportal.io](mailto:support@partnerportal.io) and they will help get your portal back online.
