# How to set up program details and partner tiers

Share how your partner program works and define commission structures that reward partners as they grow.

⏱️ **Estimated time:** 5–10 minutes | **Who can do this:** Admin only

---

## How It Works

This guide covers two independent sections under **Partner Program > Program Settings**:

| | Program Details | Partner Tiers |
|---|---|---|
| **Purpose** | Describe how your program works and how commission is structured | Reward partners with higher commissions as they hit revenue thresholds |
| **Visible to partners** | Optional — can be made public on the login page | Optional — can be made public on the sign-in page |
| **Required to run the program** | No | No |

You can set up one or both depending on your program needs.

---

## Program Details

### Step 1 — Open Program Settings

1. Go to the **Partner Program** tab on the sidebar.
2. Click on **Program Settings**.

### Step 2 — Enter Your Program Details

1. Click the **Pencil icon** in the Partner Program section.
2. Enter your program details in the text field — include how your program is structured and how commission works.
3. Click **Save**.

![Program Details edit screen](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2127852682/671276649d411e4f9ce68393fd2f/Screenshot%2B2026-03-04%2Bat%2B11_48_05-E2-80-AFAM%2B-282-29.png)

*Figure 1 — Enter your program details using the Pencil icon. Click Save when done.*

### Step 3 — Set Visibility

Choose whether your Program Details should be visible to partners.

- **Public on** — your details appear on the main login page for prospective and current partners to view.
- **Public off** — Program Details are not visible to partners.

![Program Details visibility toggle](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2127862911/d773a16de05eda95284714209ae2/Screenshot+2026-03-04+at+11_48_08%E2%80%AFAM+%282%29.png)

*Figure 2 — Toggle Public on to display your program details on the login page.*

> ℹ️ **Note:** To disable the Partner Program section entirely, toggle off the **Enabled** button.

## Partner Tiers

Partner Tiers let you reward partners with different commission structures based on revenue performance over a set period.

### Commission structures supported in tiers

- **Fixed amount**
- **Recurring percentage of revenue**
- **One-time percentage of revenue**

### Step 1 — Open Partner Tiers

1. Go to **Partner Program > Program Settings**.
2. Find the **Partner Tiers** section and click the **Pencil icon** to start editing.

### Step 2 — Define Your Base Tier

The base tier is the default commission structure all partners receive before reaching any revenue threshold. This is the starting point for every partner in your program.

Define:
- The revenue requirement
- The commission type (fixed, one-time percentage, or recurring)

### Step 3 — Add Revenue Thresholds

Add as many tiers as your program requires. For each tier, define:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Tier Name | A name to identify this level |
| Revenue threshold | The revenue amount required to reach this tier |
| Commission type | Fixed, one-time percentage, or recurring |
| Color coding | Optional — partners see their tier color when logged in |

When a partner reaches a new tier, they will see their updated tier name and color in their portal.

![Partner Tiers configuration screen](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2127798287/e4455c2a26922aa21a7bd7b7f62d/Screenshot+2026-03-04+at+11_37_01%E2%80%AFAM+%282%29.png)

*Figure 3 — Define tier names, revenue thresholds, commission types, and optional color coding.*

> ℹ️ **Note:** If Partner Tiers are marked **Public**, they will be visible in the Program tab on the sign-in page.

### Step 4 — Set Tier Calculation Frequency

Choose how often tiers are evaluated:

- **Monthly**
- **Quarterly**
- **Annually**

The system reviews all eligible revenue from Leads submitted during the selected period. Based on total revenue within that timeframe, the partner is assigned the appropriate tier and their commissions are calculated accordingly.

### Step 5 — Save Your Tier Structure

Click **Save** to apply your Partner Tier configuration.

## Troubleshooting

**Program Details are not showing on the login page.**
Make sure the **Public** toggle is turned on. If it is off, the details are saved but not visible to partners.

**Partners are not moving to a new tier after hitting the revenue threshold.**
Check the **Tier Calculation Frequency** setting. Tiers are evaluated at the end of each selected period (monthly, quarterly, or annually) — not in real time.

**I want to disable the Partner Program entirely.**
Toggle off the **Enabled** button in the Partner Program section under Program Settings.
