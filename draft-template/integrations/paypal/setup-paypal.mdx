---

The PayPal integration lets you pay partner commissions directly through PartnerPortal.io — without writing checks or logging into a separate payment system. You can send payments manually on a per-lead basis or configure automatic payment scheduling. The integration is accessible from the **Integrations** page in the left sidebar.

---

> **Note:** The PayPal integration is only available on select plans. Contact Support within your portal for more information.

---

## Requirements

### For admins

- Admin access to PartnerPortal.io
- A PayPal business account connected to PartnerPortal.io
- Payouts API enabled in your PayPal account. See [PayPal's guide on enabling the Payouts API](https://www.paypal.com/us/smarthelp/article/what-is-the-payouts-api-and-how-do-i-enable-it-on-my-account-ts2075) for instructions.

### For partners

- An active PayPal account connected to a bank account or another payment account. Partners without a PayPal account cannot receive payments through the integration. Partners who prefer to be paid through a third-party site do not need a PayPal account, but those payments will not be tracked in the portal.

---

## Connect Your PayPal Account

Before sending any payments, connect your PayPal account to the portal.

1. Log in to your portal with an Admin account.
2. Go to the **Integrations** tab in the left sidebar.
3. Under the **Payments** section, click **Enable PayPal** and log in with your PayPal account.

![Integrations page showing the Enable PayPal button under the Payments section](https://partnerportalio.intercom-attachments-1.com/i/o/660992902/1dbf96b379e67991de9eb940/image-png-Apr-16-2021-04-23-27-30-PM.png)

## Manual Payments

Send a commission payment for a specific lead at any time after your PayPal account is connected.

1. Go to the lead's detail page.
2. Click the **PayPal icon** above the transactions section.

   ![Lead detail page showing the PayPal icon above the transactions section](https://partnerportalio.intercom-attachments-1.com/i/o/660992909/a0c6141d3a032271462d58f6/image-png-Apr-16-2021-04-24-05-20-PM.png)

3. Enter the payment information and schedule the payment for when you want it to process.

   ![Payment scheduling prompt showing minimum payment information fields](https://partnerportalio.intercom-attachments-1.com/i/o/660992918/ec0c41c9bd9645042331c80d/image-png-Apr-16-2021-04-24-27-50-PM.png)

## Automatic Payments

PartnerPortal.io can generate commission payments automatically on a schedule you define.

1. Go to the **Program** page in the left sidebar.
2. Select **Payment Scheduling** and configure the payment schedule to your company's needs.

   ![Program page showing the Payment Scheduling option](https://partnerportalio.intercom-attachments-1.com/i/o/660992924/7444de50155fb4aaa6c763a3/image-png-Apr-16-2021-04-24-53-00-PM.png)

> **Note:** For commission amounts to be included automatically in each payment, automatic payments must be used together with an accounting integration. See the **Integrations** page for available options.

---

## Important Rules

- **Fund your PayPal account at least 2 business days before commissions are due.** If the account is not funded in time, the system will show an error. Once funded, go to the **Payments** tab and click **Retry** to resend the transfer.

   ![Payments tab showing the Retry button for a failed transfer](https://partnerportalio.intercom-attachments-1.com/i/o/660992935/25e99c072f4142974beb8fa5/image-png-Apr-16-2021-04-25-12-55-PM.png)

- **Payments must be approved before they are sent.** Approve payments from the **Payments** page in the left sidebar.

   ![Payments page showing pending payments awaiting approval](https://partnerportalio.intercom-attachments-1.com/i/o/660992945/81b07acaec5e10dbcdfe21f0/image-png-Apr-16-2021-04-25-50-10-PM.png)
