---
title: "Add a PayPal Email to the Portal"
description: "Learn how to add a PayPal email address to PartnerPortal.io — either as a partner managing your own account, or as an admin adding it on a partner's behalf."
---

A PayPal email address must be on file in the portal before commission payments can be sent to a partner. This guide covers both ways to add one.

---

## Before You Start

- The partner must have an active PayPal account already connected to a bank account or another payment account. If they don't have one yet, they'll need to create and set up their PayPal account before proceeding.

---

## Choose Your Approach

| | As a partner | As an admin |
|---|---|---|
| **Who does this** | The partner, in their own account | An admin, on behalf of a partner |
| **Where to go** | Account & Settings → My Account | Partners tab → partner's Details |

---

## Option A: Adding Your Own PayPal Email (Partner)

1. Log in to your partner account.
2. Go to the **Account & Settings** tab and select **My Account**.
3. Click the pencil icon in the top right to edit.
4. Enter your PayPal email in the designated field.
5. Click **Save**.

   ![Partner account settings screen showing the PayPal email field](https://partnerportalio.intercom-attachments-1.com/i/o/660994096/df6b1b07d35fc3aba98f116d/partner-20account.png)

---

## Option B: Adding a PayPal Email on Behalf of a Partner (Admin)

1. Go to the **Partners** tab and select the partner's name.
2. Click **Details**.
3. Click the pencil icon to edit.
4. Enter the PayPal email in the **Payment information** section.
5. Click **Save**.

   ![Partners tab showing a partner's detail view with the Payment information section](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/1227653824/d9b2411477d2528a7ae0ebfd4470/Screenshot+2024-10-24+at+10_29_59%E2%80%AFAM+%282%29.png)

   ![Payment information section showing the PayPal email field being filled in](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/1227653941/686fab7c4657aba0404a09f0c379/Screenshot+2024-10-24+at+10_29_34%E2%80%AFAM+%282%29.png)

Once the PayPal email is saved, the partner will be able to receive commission payments.
