---
title: "Create Leads Automatically Using Zapier"
description: "Learn how to set up a Zap that automatically creates leads in PartnerPortal.io from a form submission in HubSpot, Typeform, or another trigger app."
---

With the Zapier integration, you can automate lead creation in PartnerPortal.io from any platform that connects to Zapier — such as HubSpot forms or Typeform surveys — so that no submitted lead is entered manually.

**Who can do this:** Admins only

---

> **Important:** When testing your Zap, do not use Zapier's built-in test data. Those fields are not properly mapped and the test will not produce a valid lead. Submit a real form entry instead (a test partner's data is a good option).

---

## Before You Start

- Your Lead Registration Form in PartnerPortal.io must be fully customized before you begin. See [How to customize the Lead Registration Form](https://help.partnerportal.io/en/articles/8732193-how-to-customize-the-lead-registration-form).
- A Zapier account. If you don't have one yet, sign up at [zapier.com](https://zapier.com).
- The form or survey platform you will use as your trigger (such as HubSpot or Typeform) must be set up with all lead registration fields added.

---

## Step 1: Prepare Your Trigger Form

In your chosen form platform (HubSpot, Typeform, or similar), create or update the form to include all the fields from your Lead Registration Form in PartnerPortal.io.

> **Note:** For partner and agent fields, you are not required to include a dropdown showing all partners. You can include it if you want partners to enter their own information.

![Example form in a trigger app showing lead registration fields](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/964257847/9bfb842e59b123ca8e282b30/Screenshot+2024-02-15+at+10_30_00+AM.png)

---

## Step 2: Create a New Zap

1. Log in to Zapier and go to the **Zaps** tab.
2. Click **Make a Zap**.
3. Select your trigger app from the list of available options (for example, HubSpot or Typeform).

   ![Zapier interface showing the trigger app selection screen](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/964262652/6e071d4fd12053272b5ef2c7/Screenshot+2024-02-15+at+10_33_36+AM.png)

---

## Step 3: Configure and Test the Trigger

1. Connect your chosen trigger app to Zapier and configure the trigger event (for example, "New Form Submission" in HubSpot).
2. Submit your form with real data to give Zapier something to retrieve. Using a [test partner](https://help.partnerportal.io/en/articles/6937135-creating-a-test-partner) is recommended so that real partner data is not used.
3. Run the trigger test in Zapier to confirm it can successfully retrieve the submission.

   ![Zapier trigger test screen showing a successful form submission retrieved](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/964267870/0d1b1de1d4294d5b95c602a6/Screenshot+2024-02-15+at+10_34_40+AM.png)

---

## Step 4: Set PartnerPortal.io as the Action App

1. After the trigger is configured, choose **PartnerPortal.io** as the action app.
2. Select **Create Lead** as the action event.

---

## Step 5: Connect Your PartnerPortal.io Account

1. Follow the prompts to connect your PartnerPortal.io account to Zapier.
2. Authenticate using your account credentials. See [this guide](https://scribehow.com/shared/Finding_your_Organization_Name_for_Zapier__Wcsfl-9FSuOgHZsZ8Rsw3A) if you need help finding your PartnerPortal.io organization name.

   ![PartnerPortal.io authentication screen in Zapier](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/964269937/8a72d48298ed97a68a74b82a/Screenshot+2024-02-15+at+10_39_01+AM.png)

---

## Step 6: Map Lead Fields

Map the fields from your trigger app to the corresponding fields in PartnerPortal.io. Make sure all required lead fields are mapped before proceeding.

---

## Step 7: Test and Activate the Zap

1. Run the Zap test to verify that a lead is successfully created in PartnerPortal.io.

   ![Zapier test screen showing a successful lead creation in PartnerPortal.io](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/964276732/cfa11a918217726695aa97f3/Screenshot+2024-02-15+at+10_44_25+AM.png)

2. Check the newly created lead in PartnerPortal.io to confirm all information was captured correctly.
3. Once satisfied, click **Publish** to activate the Zap.
