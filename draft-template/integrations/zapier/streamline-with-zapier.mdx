The Zapier integration connects PartnerPortal.io to hundreds of other apps and platforms, letting you automate tasks that would otherwise require manual data entry or switching between systems.

---

## What You Can Do With It

### Automate Lead Creation

Instead of manually entering leads into the portal, you can set up a Zap to create leads automatically from any platform that connects to Zapier — such as a HubSpot form submission or a Typeform survey response. When someone fills out your lead form on an external platform, a lead is created in PartnerPortal.io without any manual step.

### Connect to Other Platforms

Zapier acts as a bridge between PartnerPortal.io and the rest of your tech stack. You can connect the portal to the tools your team already uses — for lead distribution, partner tracking, notifications, or data synchronization — without building a custom integration.

### Keep Data in Sync

When information is updated in one connected system, Zapier can reflect that change in PartnerPortal.io and vice versa, reducing the need for manual data entry across platforms.
