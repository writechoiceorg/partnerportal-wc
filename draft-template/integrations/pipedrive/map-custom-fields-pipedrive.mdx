---
title: "Map Custom Fields to Pipedrive"
description: "Learn how to map custom fields from your PartnerPortal.io lead form to your Pipedrive CRM."
---

Custom field mapping lets information submitted through your Lead Registration Form sync directly to the correct fields in Pipedrive.

**Who can do this:** Admins only

---

> **Before you start:** Custom fields must exist in **both** PartnerPortal.io and Pipedrive before they will appear in the mapping interface. Set them up in both places first:
> - [How to customize your Lead Registration Form](https://help.partnerportal.io/en/articles/8732193-how-to-customize-the-lead-registration-form)
> - [How to create custom fields in Pipedrive](https://support.pipedrive.com/en/article/custom-fields)

---

## Before You Start

- Admin access to PartnerPortal.io
- Pipedrive integration already connected and configured
- Custom fields created in both the portal Lead Registration Form and in Pipedrive

---

## Step 1: Open the Integration Mapping

1. Go to the **Integrations** tab.
2. Select **Settings** for the Pipedrive integration.
3. Click **Configure** to open the mapping page.

---

## Step 2: Map the Field

Follow the section that matches your field type.

### Single-Line Text Fields

1. In the object of your choice (deal, person, or organization), select the desired Pipedrive custom field from the dropdown. Only fields that already exist in Pipedrive will appear here.
2. Click **Add**.
3. Click the newly added field and configure the mapping:

   | Setting | Value |
   |---|---|
   | **Type** | Portal Object |
   | **Object** | Lead |
   | **Field** | The corresponding portal field from your lead form |

   ![Mapping configuration panel for a single-line text field showing Type, Object, and Field settings](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2156023365/77e745bd972edeac3dade34d018e/Screenshot+2026-03-12+at+10_12_47%E2%80%AFAM+%282%29.png)

4. Click **Save Mappings**.

---

### Picklist (Dropdown) Fields

1. Confirm the picklist field exists in both Pipedrive and the Lead Registration Form.
2. Select the custom picklist field from the object dropdown.
3. Click **Add**.
4. Click the newly added field and configure the mapping:

   | Setting | Value |
   |---|---|
   | **Type** | Portal Object |
   | **Object** | Lead |
   | **Field** | The corresponding portal field from your lead form |

5. Click **Map Values** to match each portal dropdown option to its corresponding Pipedrive value.

   ![Map Values panel for a picklist field showing portal options alongside Pipedrive dropdown values](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2156033814/d14f6b3e712afd8c387ad73fadfa/Screenshot+2026-03-12+at+10_16_43%E2%80%AFAM+%282%29.png)

6. In the mapping window, select **Map Manually** to choose exactly which portal dropdown option maps to which Pipedrive value.

   ![Map Manually option selected in the picklist mapping window](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2156047393/6ea3d0549053256b01c2e90aca50/Screenshot+2026-03-12+at+10_20_22%E2%80%AFAM+%282%29.png)

7. Click **Save** in the mapping window, then click **Save Mappings** on the main page.

---

### Fields Not on the Lead Form (Partner Data)

Some information — such as partner details — does not need to appear on the Lead Registration Form. If the destination field already exists in Pipedrive, you can map it directly without adding a new portal field.

1. In the **Deal** object, select the Pipedrive field from the dropdown.
2. Click **Add**.

   ![Deal object showing a Pipedrive field selected in the dropdown and the Add button](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2156066032/7cd19dffd4b2dedcf6baf6553f98/Screenshot%2B2026-03-10%2Bat%2B3_40_09-E2-80-AFPM%2B-282-29.png)

3. Click the newly added field and configure the mapping:

   | Setting | Value |
   |---|---|
   | **Type** | Portal Object |
   | **Object** | Partner Organization (for partner company name) or Partner User (for individual partner information) |
   | **Field** | The specific information you want to map |

   ![Mapping configuration panel showing Object set to Partner Organization with available fields](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2156078156/f2e8e3112b78f6c3237674960dbd/Screenshot+2026-03-12+at+10_28_35%E2%80%AFAM+%282%29.png)

4. Click **Save Mappings**.
