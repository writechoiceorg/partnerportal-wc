title: "Connect and Configure the Pipedrive Integration"
description: "Learn how to connect your Pipedrive CRM to PartnerPortal.io and map your pipeline stages and portal phases."
---

Connecting Pipedrive to PartnerPortal.io lets you sync deals, track pipeline updates, and give partners visibility into the sales cycle.

**Who can do this:** Admins only

---

> **Important:** The **Won** and **Lost** stages must be mapped for the integration to function correctly.

---

## Before You Start

- Admin access to PartnerPortal.io
- Access to the Pipedrive account you want to connect

---

## Step 1: Connect Your Pipedrive Account

1. Go to the **Integrations** tab in the sidebar.
2. Click **Enable Pipedrive**.
3. You will be redirected to Pipedrive. Log in if prompted, or confirm the connection if you're already logged in.
4. Once connected, click **Settings** for the Pipedrive integration.
5. Click **Configure** to begin mapping your data.

---

## Step 2: Select a Pipeline

1. In the configuration screen, locate the **Pipeline** card under the Deal object.
2. Click the card to open it.
3. Click the **Value** dropdown and select the Pipedrive pipeline you want to sync with your portal.

   ![Pipeline card showing the Value dropdown with a Pipedrive pipeline selected](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2148612167/f44c985be0478feafcf73be27b14/Screenshot+2026-03-10+at+12_37_28%E2%80%AFPM+%282%29.png)

---

## Step 3: Map Deal Stages

1. Click the **Stage** card.
2. Click **Map Values**.

   ![Stage card with the Map Values button highlighted](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2148620305/21b987494670a7b80f23ac72c365/Screenshot+2026-03-10+at+3_03_10%E2%80%AFPM+%282%29.png)

3. Map your portal phases to the corresponding Pipedrive stages.

4. In the **On Create, set Pipedrive CRM Stage to** field, choose how new deals from the portal enter your Pipedrive pipeline:

   | Option | How it works | Watch out for |
   |---|---|---|
   | **Static stage** | All new deals start in the same Pipedrive stage, regardless of the portal phase | — |
   | **Infer from mappings** | The portal determines the starting stage based on your existing phase mappings | Avoid mapping multiple portal phases to the same Pipedrive stage — this can make the starting stage inconsistent |
   | **Map manually** | You define exactly which Pipedrive stage maps to each portal phase | — |

   ![On Create field showing the three mapping method options](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2148633623/438cebbd982b3835e2399a6a9168/Screenshot+2026-03-10+at+3_06_38%E2%80%AFPM+%282%29.png)

   > **Note:** You do not need to map every stage. Map the key stages you want partners to be able to follow as a deal progresses through the sales cycle.

5. Click **Save** on the Stage card.

---

## Step 4: Map Additional Pipelines (Optional)

If you want to sync more than one pipeline, map the stages for each additional pipeline separately.

1. In the Stage mapping window, use the **pipeline selector on the left** to choose the next pipeline.

   ![Stage mapping window showing the pipeline selector on the left side](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2227665841/b09a202fd8084f8054cdc10d9325/Screenshot%2B2026-03-10%2Bat%2B3_13_33-E2-80-AFPM%2B-282-29.png)

2. Map the Pipedrive stages to the corresponding portal phases for that pipeline.
3. Repeat for each additional pipeline.

---

## Step 5: Save Your Configuration

Click **Save mappings** at the bottom of the main configuration page. Your Pipedrive integration is now active and deals will begin syncing according to the rules you have set.
