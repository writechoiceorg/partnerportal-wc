Connecting Salesforce to PartnerPortal.io lets you sync leads, map custom fields, and track opportunity stages — with real-time synchronization between the two systems.

**Who can do this:** Admins only

---

## Before You Start

- Admin access to PartnerPortal.io
- Administrative access to Salesforce
- Permissions in Salesforce to create and manage integrations

---

## Step 1: Enable the Salesforce Integration

1. Go to the **Integrations** tab in the sidebar.
2. Click **Enable Salesforce**.
3. You will be prompted to log in to your Salesforce account. Enter your credentials and grant the portal access to your Salesforce data.

---

## Step 2: Configure Integration Settings

Before mapping any objects or fields, configure the following settings to define how the integration handles your data.

![Other Settings panel showing Primary Lead Object, Duplicate Handling, and Error Handling options](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1156254479/3975e800185197a80ad8b234/other+settings.png)

**Primary Lead Object**
Select the primary lead object for your integration.

**Duplicate Handling**
Choose how the integration responds when a duplicate is detected in Salesforce:

| Option | Behavior |
|---|---|
| **Error** | An error occurs when trying to create the object if a duplicate is detected in Salesforce |
| **Adopt** | The existing record is used and tracked going forward |

The integration pulls all matching and duplicate rules from your Salesforce account.

**Error Handling**
Choose what happens if an object fails to be created in Salesforce:

| Option | Behavior |
|---|---|
| **Don't Cleanup** | Previously created objects in the process are kept even if a subsequent object fails |
| **Cleanup** | All objects created as part of the process are deleted if any one of them fails |

---

## Step 3: Map Objects and Fields

Map fields between Salesforce and the portal to define how data flows between the two systems. This includes lead names, company names, date fields, and picklists.

1. In the mapping section, use the dropdown in the grey box to select the custom object you want to map.
2. Once an object is selected, begin mapping its fields to the corresponding portal fields.
3. To add a new object, select the appropriate box next to or below your latest object.

   ![Mapping section showing the object dropdown and field mapping interface](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1156209063/df5db5af81487c30c5241dee/1.png)

   ![Expanded mapping view showing multiple objects being configured](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1156210281/23711edfa0689e76a98fc820/2.png)

For each field, configure the following options:

| Setting | Options | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| **Type** | Static Value, Portal Object, Related Object | Use **Portal Object** to map lead form fields; use **Static Value** to map the same information consistently |
| **Object** | Lead, Partner | Select based on where the data originates |
| **Field** | Any field on your lead registration form | Select the specific field to map |

   ![Field mapping panel showing Type, Object, and Field configuration options](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1156220528/7f9441a185060aad23ab705e/objects+ui.png)

   ![Example of a completed field mapping configuration](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1156249230/45e9f5ffb7552d17de8a6ccf/mappings.png)

## Step 4: Map Lead Phases to Opportunity Stages

1. Create the **Opportunity** object and find the **Stage** option.
2. Set the **Type** to **Portal Object**, the **Object** to **Lead**, and the **Field** to **Phase**.
3. Click **Map Values**.
4. Map your Salesforce opportunity stages to the corresponding portal phases. You can map them one-to-one or generalize them to control how much of your sales process partners can see.

   ![Map Values panel showing Salesforce opportunity stages being mapped to portal phases](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1156227004/18c443ef906156bb1fbc6353/map+values.png)

   ![Completed phase-to-stage mapping configuration](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1156232416/359631b6f8a9fa0cc8e0eeb3/Screenshot+2024-08-23+at+2_30_51%E2%80%AFPM.png)

## Step 5: Configure Lead Conversion

Choose how leads are converted into Salesforce objects when they are approved in the portal.

| Approach | How to configure | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Create all objects on approval** | Leave **Create on Lead Approval** checked for all objects | Teams that want leads, contacts, accounts, and opportunities created in Salesforce immediately |
| **Validate in Salesforce first** | Uncheck **Create on Lead Approval** for all objects except the lead | Teams that prefer to review leads in Salesforce before converting them to opportunities |

For the validate-first approach: after reviewing in Salesforce, convert the lead to accounts, contacts, and opportunities manually as needed.

   ![Configuration workflow panel showing the Create on Lead Approval checkbox for each object](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1156224213/36c501c8894e84d11ff15e12/configuration+workflow.png)

---

## Step 6: Save Your Configuration

Save your settings. Your Salesforce integration is now active and will begin syncing data according to the rules you have set.
