---
title: "Connect QuickBooks to Your Portal"
description: "Learn how to connect your QuickBooks account to PartnerPortal.io and map leads to their QuickBooks accounts."
---

With the QuickBooks integration, revenue information from your QuickBooks account is pulled into the portal automatically, giving partners visibility into their potential earnings.

**Who can do this:** Admins only

---

> **Important:** For a lead to be mapped to QuickBooks, that lead's customer must already have an account in QuickBooks. You can create QuickBooks accounts at any time — leads can be mapped after approval, and the integration will pick up any new customer revenue from that point on.

---

## Before You Start

- Admin access to PartnerPortal.io
- A QuickBooks account
- Customer accounts created in QuickBooks for each lead you want to map

---

## Step 1: Connect Your QuickBooks Account

1. Go to the **Integrations** tab.
2. Click **Enable QuickBooks**.

   ![Integrations tab showing the Enable QuickBooks button](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1078344018/7f318f3ab061d6599eb08023/Screenshot+2024-06-11+at+9_19_59%E2%80%AFAM+%282%29.png)

3. Log in to your QuickBooks account and connect it to your portal.

   ![QuickBooks login and connection screen](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1078344186/e56743357d033c2b44987b23/Screenshot+2024-06-11+at+9_20_08%E2%80%AFAM+%282%29.png)

---

## Step 2: Map Your Leads

1. Once connected, click **Map Leads**.

   ![Portal screen showing the Map Leads button after QuickBooks is connected](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1078344542/3b7d70704052bfbe8368e959/Screenshot+2024-06-11+at+9_20_22%E2%80%AFAM+%282%29.png)

2. Map each lead to their corresponding QuickBooks customer account.

   ![Lead mapping screen showing leads being matched to QuickBooks accounts](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1078343415/29f7dd50d62b4b84e14cba54/Screenshot+2024-06-11+at+9_20_38%E2%80%AFAM+%282%29.png)
