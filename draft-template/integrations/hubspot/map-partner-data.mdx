---
title: "Map Partner Data to HubSpot"
description: "Learn how to map partner company and partner user information from PartnerPortal.io to your HubSpot deal properties."
---

You can map partner information — such as company details or the submitting user's contact data — directly to fields on your HubSpot deal. Once configured, this mapping happens automatically whenever a deal is created.

**Who can do this:** Admins only

---

## Before You Start

- Admin access to PartnerPortal.io
- A custom field already created as a HubSpot deal property to receive the partner data. If you haven't created it yet, follow [HubSpot's guide to creating and editing properties](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/properties/create-and-edit-properties) first.

---

## Step 1: Open the HubSpot Integration Settings

1. Go to the **Integrations** tab and open your HubSpot configuration.
2. In the **Deal** object, scroll to the **custom field add-on section** at the bottom.

---

## Step 2: Select and Add the Field

1. Click the dropdown to view all custom fields available in your pipeline.
2. Select the field you want to receive the partner data.
3. Click **Add**.
4. Click on the newly added field to open its configuration.

![HubSpot Deal object showing a custom field selected in the add-on section dropdown](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/1219700467/819da92e87cc80bcd2effc84e9ea/Screenshot+2024-10-17+at+10_49_40%E2%80%AFAM+%282%29.png)

---

## Step 3: Configure the Mapping

Set the **Type** to **Portal object**, then choose the **Object** based on what you want to map:

| Object | What it maps |
|---|---|
| **Partner** | Partner company details |
| **Partner User** | pp.io ID, First Name, Last Name, Email, or Phone number of the submitting user |

In the **Field** dropdown, select the specific portal field you want mapped to this HubSpot property.

![Mapping configuration panel showing Type set to Portal object, Object set to Partner User, and a Field selected](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/1219700667/d2abab83023e2e1d955cc2932f84/Screenshot+2024-10-17+at+10_49_57%E2%80%AFAM+%282%29.png)

---

## Step 4: Save Your Settings

Click **Save**. Partner data will now map automatically to your HubSpot deal each time a deal is created.
