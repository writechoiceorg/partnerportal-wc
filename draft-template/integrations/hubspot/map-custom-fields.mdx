---
title: "Map Custom Fields to HubSpot"
description: "Learn how to map custom text and picklist fields from your PartnerPortal.io lead form to your HubSpot deal properties."
---

Custom fields let you pass partner-submitted data directly into your HubSpot deal. This guide walks you through mapping both single-line text fields and picklist (drop-down) fields.

**Who can do this:** Admins only

---

> **Before you start:** The custom field must already exist in HubSpot as a deal property before it will appear in the portal's mapping interface. If you haven't created it yet, follow [HubSpot's guide to creating and editing properties](https://knowledge.hubspot.com/properties/create-and-edit-properties) first.

---

## Before You Start

Make sure you have the following in place before beginning:

- Admin access to PartnerPortal.io
- The custom field already created as a HubSpot deal property
- The custom field added to your Lead Registration Form (see Step 1 below)

---

## Step 1: Add the Custom Field to Your Lead Form

Before you can map a field to HubSpot, it needs to be active on your Lead Registration Form.

1. Go to the **Leads** tab under **Accounts & Settings**.
2. Open the **Lead Registration Form** and click the pencil icon to edit it.
3. Add your custom field.
4. Check the **Active** box so the field appears on the form.
5. Check **Required** if you want partners to fill it in before submitting.

![Lead Registration Form editor showing a custom field being added with the Active checkbox enabled](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2024390784/e6bbcda6610f25bfc085a2b5794b/Screenshot+2026-02-03+at+3_51_11%E2%80%AFPM.png)

---

## Step 2: Open the HubSpot Integration Settings

1. Go to the **Integrations** tab.
2. Select **Settings** under HubSpot.
3. Click **Configure**.
4. Scroll to the **Deal** object and find the custom field section at the bottom.

![HubSpot configuration screen showing the Deal object with the custom fields section expanded](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/1219629765/d0e396a197e56c42292a8e4ab494/Screenshot+2024-10-17+at+10_47_03%E2%80%AFAM+%282%29.png)

---

## Step 3: Map the Field

The mapping steps vary slightly depending on the field type. Follow the section that matches your field.

### Single-Line Text Fields

1. In the **Deal** object, select your custom field from the dropdown.

   ![Dropdown showing available custom fields in the Deal object](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/1219633138/d99c6d3cc8acd0b4b5dc3b939775/Screenshot+2024-10-17+at+11_42_01%E2%80%AFAM+%282%29.png)

2. Click **Add**.

   ![Custom field added to the mapping list](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/1219635160/a37bc30234c193e83bbad18796db/Screenshot+2024-10-17+at+11_43_42%E2%80%AFAM+%282%29.png)

3. Click the newly added field and configure the mapping:

   | Setting | Value |
   |---|---|
   | **Type** | Portal object |
   | **Object** | Lead |
   | **Field** | The custom portal field you want to map |

   ![Mapping configuration panel for a single-line text field](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/1219635652/2b318bd0bea9399347f7f2228835/Screenshot+2024-10-17+at+11_43_12%E2%80%AFAM+%282%29.png)

---

### Picklist (Drop-Down) Fields

Make sure the picklist field exists in both your HubSpot deal properties and your Lead Registration Form before starting.

1. Select the custom picklist field from the dropdown in the **Deal** object.
2. Click **Add**.
3. Click the newly added field and configure the mapping:

   | Setting | Value |
   |---|---|
   | **Type** | Portal object |
   | **Object** | Lead |
   | **Field** | The custom portal field you want to map |

   ![Mapping configuration panel for a picklist field](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/1219642905/bb9635d9b74da0678edb72f900a3/Screenshot+2024-10-17+at+11_44_10%E2%80%AFAM+%282%29.png)

4. Click **Map Values** to match each PartnerPortal.io lead field option to the corresponding HubSpot drop-down value.

   ![Map Values panel showing pp.io lead options mapped to HubSpot drop-down values](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/1219643710/60282c0869dd7a1131a557117201/Screenshot+2024-10-17+at+11_44_34%E2%80%AFAM+%282%29.png)

---

## Step 4: Save Your Settings

Click **Save** when you have finished mapping all fields.

---

## Troubleshooting

**My custom field doesn't appear in the mapping dropdown.**
The field must exist as a HubSpot deal property before it shows up in the portal. Go to HubSpot and create the property first, then return to the mapping screen.

**The field is in HubSpot but still not appearing.**
Make sure the field is also added to your Lead Registration Form in PartnerPortal.io and that the **Active** box is checked (Step 1).
