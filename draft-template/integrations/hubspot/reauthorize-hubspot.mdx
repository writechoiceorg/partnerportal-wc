---
title: "Reauthorize Your HubSpot Connection"
description: "Follow these steps to reauthorize your HubSpot connection in PartnerPortal.io without losing your historical data."
---

Occasional API updates from HubSpot may require you to reauthorize your connection to PartnerPortal.io.

**Who can do this:** Admins only

---

## When do you need this?

You will need this guide if you have received an error or a request to reauthorize your HubSpot connection.

## Why this happens

HubSpot API changes and updates can invalidate your existing authorization, requiring you to reconnect the app.

---

> **Warning:** Do not disconnect the HubSpot app. If you disconnect, all previously connected leads will no longer sync to HubSpot, and you would need to reconnect each lead individually to restore the historical data.

---

## Steps

1. Log in to your partner portal.

2. In the left-hand sidebar, go to the **Integrations** tab. Inside the HubSpot window, select **Settings**.

   ![PartnerPortal.io Integrations tab showing the HubSpot window with the Settings option](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2377232756/0de70b873279a901cd43081c1452/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=ba0d67c86ab66ca57ff2f4e65eb63f05fdea4daa35d762daf512b24b8c3bd33b&amp;req=diMgEct9n4ZaX%2FMW1HO4zcX2EuuruSZeJo4oTLLCxap4tVBmCnVGiAMODq%2FK%0AJVIS3%2BqwGTszyTMP5Mk%3D%0A)

3. From the Settings view, click **Reauthorize**. Do not click Disconnect.

   ![HubSpot Settings view showing the Reauthorize button](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2377233691/87354b6e0a8543005cccf941e3b5/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=83a08bb956402a033ba90c391ef095941cedc5071f0981bc89586a86b4373b9a&amp;req=diMgEct9nodWWPMW1HO4za6We1fRx18SvslAPfZz1zUNhA8kj1An9VSn1EHF%0AUMzYtiXKPNsNBnaHNlI%3D%0A)

4. Confirm you are reauthorizing into the correct HubSpot account.

   *(Note: If you only have access to one HubSpot account, you may not see this screen.)*

   ![HubSpot account selection screen during reauthorization](https://partnerportalio.intercom-attachments-1.com/i/o/660968355/4cc98970f8d582523a8c60f5/Screen-20Shot-202022-02-17-20at-209.15.14-20AM.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=bb8ba5bf18cc7314938a93bcfaf62fc8411e2c7812d008f25cb6dad05249fe13&amp;req=ciYnH892noRaFb4f3HP0gGmP1NaYewKwRwWx26gvJTCUqV3nfF7nZfoiA%2FPg%0AhqoJNCNxfe01D4dHRw%3D%3D%0A)

5. Click **Connect App**.

   ![HubSpot Connect App confirmation screen](https://partnerportalio.intercom-attachments-1.com/i/o/660968361/3d8c5a0e64be15d925a60538/Screen-20Shot-202022-02-17-20at-209.19.50-20AM.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=72f250359b3154745cd58cef851f3ded24e632b9d363fc460b086745552b32de&amp;req=ciYnH892nodeFb4f3HP0gAba7fWAuHYyPCzEfmjF5dPst25qx2j4n7rvNvQe%0AnsPbUGbuhjJu%2Bvt7sw%3D%3D%0A)

## Confirm it worked

A success notification will appear on the screen confirming the reauthorization is complete.


---

## If this didn't work

If you run into any issues while reauthorizing, contact [support@partnerportal.io](mailto:support@partnerportal.io) and the team can assist.
