---
title: "Enable Reseller Permissions for Partners"
description: "Learn how to enable Two-Way Sync and Reseller Permissions so that partners can update deal stages in PartnerPortal.io on your behalf."
---

Reseller Permissions allow partners to update portal phases directly, and those changes sync back to HubSpot automatically. This reduces manual work for your team when partners are actively managing deals.

**Who can do this:** Portal owners and admins only

---

> **Important:** Reseller Permissions are only available with the HubSpot CRM integration. This feature is not compatible with other CRMs.

---

## Before You Start

- Owner or Admin access to PartnerPortal.io
- HubSpot integration already connected
- Two-Way Sync must be enabled and Portal Phases must be mapped before you can activate Reseller Permissions for a partner (both covered in this guide)

---

## Step 1: Enable Two-Way Sync

Two-Way Sync allows partners to edit Phase, Close Value, and Close Date in the portal, with changes syncing back to HubSpot.

1. Go to the **Integrations** tab, select **HubSpot Settings**, and click **Configure**.
2. Scroll down to the **Other Settings** section and enable **Two-Way Sync**.

   ![HubSpot Other Settings section with the Two-Way Sync toggle enabled](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2374978066/7f6f7b90da0337eb38a7f662bab4/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=d9133dd0e9f380952b11c06d1663ed54bdf508690afbf938bd0f3f09a7746480&amp;req=diMgEsB5lYFZX%2FMW1HO4zRD0%2BdL1ggZUXA5WLV%2F25IRmWIeu97VVJJjOHmR1%0Ajs4NecIGEYjVyiRBXHY%3D%0A)

## Step 2: Map Portal Phases to HubSpot Deal Stages

With Two-Way Sync enabled, you need to map which HubSpot deal stage each portal phase corresponds to, and vice versa.

1. Scroll up to the **Deal Stage** field and click **Map Values**.
2. In the **When Portal Phase is changed** section, select which HubSpot stage a deal should move to when the portal phase changes (for example, Lost → Closed Lost).
3. Below that, map which portal phase should update when a HubSpot deal stage changes.

   ![Deal Stage mapping panel showing portal phases mapped to HubSpot deal stages in both directions](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2375017913/5f21cff576a42c12a9d6a438a020/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=75752ae2313ad56d5c56a01ef04c2f609f3bfdf23b900e1d51c064f6f39f1a6e&amp;req=diMgE8l%2FmoheWvMW1HO4zfkBXviYatNCmlpI%2FWXq38zWj1i2dRNdFvZND6Io%0A%2F2Nt%2FPmbTRurt2NhAis%3D%0A)

4. Before saving, make sure the **On Create set HubSpot Deal Stage to** field is also mapped.
5. Save your settings.

---

## Step 3: Enable Reseller Permissions for a Partner

1. Go to the **Partners** tab and select the partner you want to enable Reseller Permissions for.
2. Click **More Details** and scroll to the **Permissions** section.
3. Toggle **Reseller Permissions** on or off.

   ![Partner detail view showing the Permissions section with the Reseller Permissions toggle](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2375021323/8301c50230854ef740ff9dcd2593/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=77b58fe04cf92e09e776d3cdeb5ac31e15c38f6b4f105751a2b5b339ef97bd19&amp;req=diMgE8l8nIJdWvMW1HO4zb8E8LFwJlStdszg%2Fm8cCWZwXYViJ0wgK%2FI0Wt3U%0Awsvj4YxNF3G5iJxZ%2FvI%3D%0A)

Once enabled, the partner and their users can update Portal Phases on your behalf.
