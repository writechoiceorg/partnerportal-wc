**Who can do this:** Portal Admin, Portal Owner

Attaching a file to a partner's account ensures only that partner has visibility on it — for example, contracts or agreements.

<Callout type="warning">
  Only **Partner Owners** can see the Partner Files section on the partner side. The section is not visible to partners with a different role.
</Callout>

---

## Attach a file to a partner account

1. Open the partner's account in your portal.
2. Locate the Partner Files section.
3. Upload the file.
4. Use the **toggle** to make the file visible to the partner — switch it to the right and the file will appear in the partner's account only.

<Callout type="info">
  File size may not exceed **100MB**.
</Callout>
