## Before you start

The Partner dropdown displays only partner companies in **Active** status. If a partner does not appear in the dropdown, their account is not currently active.

This typically means they:

- Have not completed registration
- Are still in **Pending** status
- Have been **Declined**
- Have been **Suspended**
