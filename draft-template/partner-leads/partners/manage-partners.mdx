You can view all partners from the **Partners** page in the sidebar. This page displays important partner details, including registration status, earnings, commission percentage, and contact information.

## Verify a new partner application

When a new partner registers, they must be approved before they can submit leads.

1. Go to **Partners** in the sidebar, or view pending partners at the bottom of your dashboard.
2. Filter by **Pending**.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2172553307/9600d5e4b872f14f1d76adf2febd/Screenshot+2026-03-17+at+12_21_16%E2%80%AFPM+%282%29.png?expires=1778101200&signature=00bf4b3b1343cb2c6a0b55aac98fa7fb3cf911d172895a4456cafa9280119801&req=diEgFMx7noJfXvMW1HO4zToXbOkkc7tkn3q7vlnViwEOsLMXaTamqhz%2BNmhY%0ATXr3%0A)

3. Click the partner you want to review.
4. Locate **Status** in the top-left corner and open the dropdown.
5. Change the status to **Approved** or **Declined**.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2172560544/b78dd67075e4e487ef409e980ad2/Screenshot+2026-03-17+at+12_23_39%E2%80%AFPM+%282%29.png?expires=1778101200&signature=42f42e268aaca406e4ebb0ce218b2e540565ab6baa8a0f2cf93fcd4aab9e8fda&req=diEgFMx4nYRbXfMW1HO4zXwN%2FIk7hEozzu25LS4IqMVBvFcCz4xPN05QeUB9%0AVLoKsm8vOCxpwc%2FC%2FUk%3D%0A)

Both actions trigger an email notification to the partner with their updated status.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Approved** | The partner is accepted into your program and can begin submitting leads. |
| **Declined** | The partner is not accepted and will not have portal access or lead submission ability. |

---

## Update an existing partner's status

After approval, a partner's status can still be updated at any time.

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Active** | The partner has full access to the portal and can submit leads. |
| **Declined** | The partner's portal access has been removed. |
| **Suspended** | The partner is under review and cannot access the portal. |

<Callout type="info">
  Suspended partners do **not** count toward your total partner count.
</Callout>

For a step-by-step guide on suspending accounts, refer to [this article](https://help.partnerportal.io/en/articles/9006216-how-to-suspend-an-account).

Selecting a partner record gives you access to additional information, including:

- Registered users
- Submitted leads
- Upcoming payments
- Payment history
