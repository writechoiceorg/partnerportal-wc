**Who can do this:** Partner Owner, Partner Admin

## Before you start

Each team member receives their own unique login and can only access the portal after being invited.

---

## Add a team member

1. Go to **Portal Settings** in the sidebar and select **Team**.
2. Click **Add Team Member** in the upper-right corner.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388467511/553f7b86b6918988a0607576b116/image.png?expires=1779111900&signature=6865ef461847d0756c8800ca9f6a05395c06ce610bb4eff60ad773a65b7236df&req=diMvHs14moReWPMW1HO4zcDyXQ8bZCoUh5XILrpGLK6FjuMucLJek6lUeN7e%0AGNs5%0A)

3. Enter the following required information:

   - First Name
   - Last Name
   - Email Address
   - Phone Number — must be 11 digits, including country code.

   <Callout type="info">
     All fields are required to send the invitation. If you do not have an active phone number, any 11-digit number can be used.
   </Callout>

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388468280/4200f8e6f1a951f677557eecdb7a/image.png?expires=1779111900&signature=bc0d97de9177a68393808bf5ac7409ce4a8ffc09caaee36a9b864cba03b6d73b&req=diMvHs14lYNXWfMW1HO4zVyxPxMpsUydfMOiIbQqYY9gOc1Ygu7nvyLeuGFd%0AXlccj%2F6fE8HETZcFHjw%3D%0A)

4. Submit the form to send the invitation.

Once the team member is added, you can adjust their role and permissions to determine their visibility in the portal.

---

## What happens after the invitation is sent

The team member will receive an email to join your organization. They must follow the link in the email to create a password and complete their account setup.

<Callout type="warning">
  The invitation link expires after a limited time. If it expires before registration is completed, the team member will need to request a [password reset link](https://help.partnerportal.io/en/articles/8694347-how-to-reset-password).
</Callout>
