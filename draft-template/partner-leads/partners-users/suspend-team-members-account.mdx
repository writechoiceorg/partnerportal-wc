**Who can do this:** Partner Account Owner

---

## Suspend a team member

1. Go to **Portal Settings** in the sidebar and select **Team**.
2. Find the team member and click the **edit** button next to their name.
3. In the pop-up window, open the **Status** dropdown — it should currently show **Active**.
4. Select **Suspended** and click **Save**.

The team member is now suspended. They will receive an email notifying them of the change and will no longer have access to the portal.

---

## Reactivate a suspended account

To restore access, follow the same steps and set the team member's status back to **Active**.
