## Update a partner user's role

1. Go to **Partners** in the sidebar and select **All Partners**.
2. Select the partner company.
3. Scroll down to the **Partner Users** section.
4. Click the **Edit** button next to the partner user's name.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388486403/b6605fb195370802eb63c9eba532/image.png?expires=1779111900&signature=92bee33a481dc889f5a9f034a0d1fa74d5b1c7e4e2f4100bcb65efbcfde5a2a4&req=diMvHs12m4VfWvMW1HO4zaHvZ8XNaR02xNP2ITzfO3kn%2FkuD86ZS0AdgHBF%2B%0A8v4uBDY1AXYXe50WY%2Bk%3D%0A)

5. In the pop-up window, select the desired role or permission.
6. Click **Save**.
