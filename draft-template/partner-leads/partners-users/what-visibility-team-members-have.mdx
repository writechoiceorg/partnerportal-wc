---
title: "What Visibility Do Team Members Have?"
description: "Understand the different roles available for team members and what each role can access in the portal."
---

As a Partner Owner, you control what each team member can see and do in the portal by assigning them a role.

---

## Roles and permissions

There are three roles available for team members:

**Partner Owner**
- Can configure the organization.
- Can see and work all leads submitted by anyone.
- Can promote other team members to Owner or demote them.

**Partner Admin**
- Can see and work all leads submitted by anyone.
- Cannot configure organization information.
- Can manage Partner Users.

**Partner User**
- Can see and work only the leads they submitted, or leads submitted on their behalf by team members or Partner Admins.
- Cannot see leads submitted by others.

---

## Opportunities

All team members, regardless of role, have access to the **Opportunities** tab and can view all opportunities sent by the portal admin team.

---

## How to update a team member's role

To update a team member's role, go to the **Team** tab in the sidebar and click the **edit** button next to their name. Select the desired role in the pop-up window and click **Save**.
