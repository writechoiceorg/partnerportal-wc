**Who can do this:** Portal Admin, Portal Owner

## Before you start

- The portal does not support duplicate email addresses. A single email cannot be attached to more than one partner or partner user account. See [Duplicate email addresses](#duplicate-email-addresses) below if this applies.

---

## Add a partner user

1. Go to the **Partners** tab and select the respective partner.
2. Scroll to the bottom of the page and click **Add Partner User**.
3. Enter the following required information:

   - First Name
   - Last Name
   - Email Address
   - Phone Number — must include the country code and be 11 digits long.

   <Callout type="info">
     If a valid phone number is unavailable, any 11-digit number can be used temporarily.
   </Callout>

4. Click **Submit** and wait a few seconds for confirmation.

A success message will appear at the top of the screen confirming that the invitation has been sent.

---

## What happens after the invitation is sent

The team member will receive an email with instructions to set up their password and complete account registration.

<Callout type="warning">
  The invitation link is time-sensitive. Inform the team member that they have a limited time to complete their account setup. If the link expires, they will need to request a password reset by following [these steps](https://help.partnerportal.io/en/articles/8694347-how-to-reset-password).
</Callout>

---

## Duplicate email addresses

The portal does not support duplicate emails. If a partner user has already registered with an email under a different partner account, the system will prevent adding a duplicate.

**To resolve a duplicate email:**

- The duplicate account must be updated to an invalid email address. This can only be done by the PartnerPortal.io support team.
- Contact support at [support@partnerportal.io](mailto:support@partnerportal.io) with the duplicate partner company name and the user's email address.

Once the duplicate account is updated, the correct account can be registered without issues.
