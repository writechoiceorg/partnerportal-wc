## View your leads

Go to the **Leads** tab, accessible from the sidebar or the main dashboard.

The **All Leads** tab displays all current and past leads submitted by any partner. You can filter leads by:

- **Status**: the lead's current status within the portal
- **Lead Name**: lead or company name
- **Partner**: who submitted the lead
- **Submission Date**: the date the submission was done.
- **Type**: options depend on your portal setup

<Callout type="info">
  All submitted leads are placed in **Pending** status and must be approved or declined by the admin team. Partners receive notifications when the lead status changes to Approved, Active, Declined, or Expired.
</Callout>

## View lead details

Click on a lead to open its detail page. From there you can see:

- Comments left by staff members
- Payment information
- Transaction log

## Edit a lead

Only admin team members can edit lead details.

1. Open the lead's detail page.
2. Click the **pencil icon** to make the appropriate fields editable.
3. Make your changes, including updating lead information or the commission value.
4. Click **Submit** to save your edits.
