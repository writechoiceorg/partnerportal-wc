# How to reset your password

Request a password reset link and create a new password to access your portal.

⏱️ **Estimated time:** 2–3 minutes | **Who can do this:** All users

---

> ⚠️ **Warning:** Make sure **pop-ups are enabled** in your browser before starting. The password reset flow opens in a pop-up window — if pop-ups are blocked, the process will not work.

---

## Before You Start

- These same steps apply if you were **invited to the portal but have not yet created your password**.
- You will need access to the email address associated with your portal account.

---

## Step 1 — Go to Your Portal Login Page

Open your portal login page in your browser. The URL follows this format:

```
companyname.partnerportal.io
```

---

## Step 2 — Enter Your Email Address

1. Enter your **email address** on the login screen.
2. Click **Next**.

![Login screen with email field](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2159917747/69d3a7a99abe456be70d7efbc71a/Screenshot%2B2023-12-11%2Bat%2B9_10_52-E2-80-AFAM.png)

*Figure 1 — Enter your email address and click Next to continue.*

---

## Step 3 — Select Forgot Password

In the pop-up window that appears, select **Forgot Password**.

![Pop-up with Forgot Password option](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2159934440/1bbb627d2395b1e4f27e9e356628/Screenshot-2B2023-12-11-2Bat-2B9_12_17-E2-80-AFAM.png)

*Figure 2 — Select Forgot Password in the pop-up to request a reset link.*

---

## Step 4 — Request the Reset Link

Your email address will be pre-filled automatically. Click **Continue** to send the reset link to your inbox.

![Continue button to request reset link](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2159938187/385ff3d3e3c796a0b26c3151ebde/Screenshot-2B2023-12-11-2Bat-2B9_13_39-E2-80-AFAM.png)

*Figure 3 — Your email is pre-filled. Click Continue to request the password reset email.*

> 💡 **Tip:** Double-check that your email address is spelled correctly before clicking Continue.

---

## Step 5 — Check Your Inbox

A password reset email will be sent to your inbox. Open the email and follow the link to create your new password.

![Password reset confirmation screen](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2159940063/7ce0925f0a1ec9e9d6faf42829be/Screenshot-2B2023-12-11-2Bat-2B9_15_43-E2-80-AFAM.png)

*Figure 4 — Confirmation that the reset email has been sent.*

---

## Troubleshooting

**The pop-up window did not appear after clicking Next.**
Pop-ups are blocked in your browser. Enable pop-ups for your portal URL and try again. Check your browser's settings or address bar for a blocked pop-up notification.

**I did not receive the reset email.**
Check your **Spam** or **Junk** folder. If the email is not there, return to your portal login page, clear your browser's cookies and cache, and repeat the steps above.

**The reset link in the email is not working.**
Reset links expire after a set period. Return to your portal login page and request a new link.

**I still cannot reset my password after trying the steps above.**
Contact the PartnerPortal support team at [support@partnerportal.io](mailto:support@partnerportal.io) for help.
