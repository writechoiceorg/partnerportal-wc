---

import { Callout } from "@/components/ui/callout";

<Callout type="info">
  Account deletion is not currently supported. Suspending an account restricts portal access without removing it.
</Callout>

All suspended users receive an email notification informing them of the suspension.

---

## Suspend a team member

**Who can do this:** Portal Admin, Portal Owner

1. Go to **Accounts & Settings** in the sidebar and select the **Team** tab.
2. Find the team member and click the **edit** button next to their name.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1169078183/a7550ca804f5243d16e3b88a/Screenshot+2024-09-04+at+2_52_08%E2%80%AFPM+%282%29.png?expires=1778093100&signature=5b210fde92e1cc86bec55f0388b0955d8c4a0c3221958964e0c8d0fe91276721&req=dSEhH8l5lYBXWvMW1HO4zU8drJuguChVuw3eKsWpvfAuNvq4IYJ6OHtsFzQ2%0AWvvKm7gZb9P4HGvVJ5g%3D%0A)

3. In the pop-up window, open the **Status** dropdown — it should show **Active**.
4. Select **Suspend** and save.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1169077299/e779c50f26ac7df1c3dd3769/Screenshot+2024-09-04+at+4_10_45%E2%80%AFPM+%282%29.png?expires=1778093100&signature=9f75049e8ab34e51fdb3a44e5dcce8612fcbbcb131d753f2cd610d0ed3fe17a5&req=dSEhH8l5moNWUPMW1HO4zUAYpiGy9piwKCpJUQtwDVDfpOarKOrNCfvSvwqH%0AcuATpCkrRK%2FhH0C2Dx4%3D%0A)

## Suspend a partner account

**Who can do this:** Admin team

1. Go to the **Partners** tab and click on the respective partner account.
2. In the top-right area, open the **Status** dropdown — it should show **Active**.
3. Select **Suspend** and save.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/977032716/1babc9607c506dfd4bf5d228/Screenshot+2024-02-28+at+3_28_21+PM.png?expires=1778093100&signature=21ccee288ac4f763450d12c5e2d41d701b2e9ab3c336bd794c4a9a19241cd85d&req=fScgFsp8moBZFb4f3HP0gCc7UZx26AGHH5dXDB3%2BYy1UDDM4hg35GrdwnkWg%0AFhKmRqmjHNAKiJlI3g%3D%3D%0A)

<Callout type="info">
  Both **Decline** and **Suspend** status will restrict the partner's access to the portal.
</Callout>

## Suspend a partner user

**Who can do this:** Admin team

1. Go to the **Partners** tab and select the respective partner account.
2. Scroll to the bottom and find the **Partner Users** section.
3. Click the **edit** button next to the partner user's name.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1169076755/d0d480ae0e7e2bffba834b88/Screenshot+2024-09-04+at+3_46_54%E2%80%AFPM+%282%29.png?expires=1778093100&signature=5ccf271b0f3462c1697c1bd7bf823fc7daa5848748e7391ce2d2ad4d469a0070&req=dSEhH8l5m4ZaXPMW1HO4zeYsocMF9xjPc7Qihhi5VWPHPmGJ6rmTG8UC9GYv%0AXolaxkFB5%2F25V3ANVqU%3D%0A)

4. In the pop-up window, open the **Status** dropdown — it should show **Active**.
5. Select **Suspend** and save.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/1169076218/1a759bf12bc2aa2f9ebceac0/Screenshot+2024-09-04+at+3_48_21%E2%80%AFPM+%282%29.png?expires=1778093100&signature=810b31c2adacae93b5f0a10e71c40117ae84a9358a1156a8d42e73fbc7b16c53&req=dSEhH8l5m4NeUfMW1HO4zX8%2FyrYtHbkoJQ%2Bm2mnsR9G2CSKPS%2FYcQR2oAxPg%0AzksAr8MkNgdagBzYdsQ%3D%0A)
