## Update a team member's role

1. Go to **Accounts & Settings** in the sidebar and select the **Team** tab.
2. Find the team member and click the **edit** button next to their name.
3. In the pop-up window, select the desired role.
4. Click **Save**.

---

## Available roles

| Role | Access |
|---|---|
| **Portal Owner** | Full access to all aspects of the portal. Can manage all users, promote team members to Owner, or demote them. |
| **Portal Admin** | Access to most portal features. Can manage other Admins, Team Members, and Partner Users. |
| **Team Member** | Can work leads in the portal. Cannot issue payments, manage integrations, or configure most portal settings. Can manage Partner Users. |
