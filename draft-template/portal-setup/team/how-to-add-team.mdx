A team member is someone within your company or organization who needs access to manage or work within your portal.

**Who can do this:** Portal Owner

---

## Add a team member

1. Go to **Accounts & Settings** in the sidebar.
2. Select the **Team** sub-tab.
3. Click **Add Team Member** in the upper-right corner.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2172518581/ee9a13f204acca609ab6d20ce139/Screenshot%2B2024-09-04%2Bat%2B2_52_07-E2-80-AFPM%2B-282-29.png?expires=1778093100&signature=45da18c5b1edb314065939d0ae6d230ccc18627c781bbe28d41aee53fdd56be6&req=diEgFMx%2FlYRXWPMW1HO4zYyYpohUVnVkuHDjUsl6yqVael6CLv3vRNTWQ4bp%0AnK3N%0A)

4. Enter the required information:

   - First Name
   - Last Name
   - Email Address
   - Phone Number

   <Callout type="info">
     If you do not have a valid phone number for a staff member, any 11-digit number can be used.
   </Callout>

5. Click **Save** to create their account.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2172520694/9a97d175ddb6f4fb577e2581eb0b/Screenshot%2B2024-09-04%2Bat%2B2_52_08-E2-80-AFPM%2B-282-29.png?expires=1778093100&signature=dec7f42cec7a3a656d017c61c38ecae98063d566259b2c592dbc283062675b3d&req=diEgFMx8nYdWXfMW1HO4zdWCaMuouYhANkP3baLo0pmX49nuu92j2F9nLmci%0AsDis9UQvXuQqNmZdcyc%3D%0A)
