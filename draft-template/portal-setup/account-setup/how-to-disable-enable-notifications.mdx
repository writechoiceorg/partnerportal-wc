## Manage your notification settings

These steps apply to both Admin team members and Partners.

1. Go to **Accounts & Settings** in the sidebar.
2. Select the **My Account** sub-tab.
3. Locate the **Notifications** section.
4. Toggle off any setting to disable it, or toggle on to enable it.
