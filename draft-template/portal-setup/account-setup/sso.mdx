PartnerPortal.io supports Single Sign-On (SSO) to simplify login and improve security for your team and partners. SSO is powered by Auth0, which allows you to connect a wide range of identity providers.

---

## Supported identity providers

Because SSO is powered by Auth0, you can integrate with any identity provider supported by Auth0, including:

- Okta
- Google Workspace
- Microsoft Azure AD
- LDAP
- And many others

<Callout type="info">
  Okta is one of the most commonly used providers and is fully supported.
</Callout>

For a complete list of supported providers, refer to [Auth0's Enterprise Identity Providers documentation](https://auth0.com/docs/authenticate/identity-providers/enterprise-identity-providers).

---

## What SSO provides

**Enhanced security** — reduces password usage and lowers the risk of phishing attacks.

**Improved user experience** — users log in once to access the portal without managing multiple credentials.

**Centralized user management** — control user access directly through your identity provider.
