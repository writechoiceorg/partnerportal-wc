## Update billing information

1. Go to **Account & Settings** in the sidebar.
2. Select the **Billing** tab.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2172392059/cd96214d8fa7492bb627f22bebf3/Screenshot+2026-03-17+at+11_33_14%E2%80%AFAM+%282%29.png?expires=1778092200&signature=b875a465d0d7ecde8fa49a11d2f207071d2375be9245d9f3e5ea641a158055ae&req=diEgFMp3n4FaUPMW1HO4zRti6IRCAi8rxw68jYUpsi3TOl%2BW2Vm17nFYoe0v%0ABhg7OVQIbcYWdwt3N3U%3D%0A)

From here, you can:

- View your current plan details, including billing cycle and account limits.
- Add or update your credit card information.
- Enable auto-pay for future billing.

## Update company information

### Subdomain (your portal URL)

Your subdomain is the default URL partners use to access your portal.

- If your subdomain is `companyname`, your portal URL will be: `companyname.partnerportal.io`

<Callout type="info">
  This is not a fully custom domain. All standard portals include **partnerportal.io** in the URL.
</Callout>

### Custom domain (optional)

If you prefer a fully branded experience, you can set up a custom domain — for example, `partners.yourcompanyname.com`.

To set a custom domain:

1. Enter your desired domain in the **Custom Domain** field.
2. Follow the instructions provided to complete setup.

<Callout type="info">
  You must enter a fully qualified domain, for example: `partners.yourcompanyname.com`.
</Callout>

### Support email

You can add a support email address for your partners. This email:

- Appears in all portal notification emails.
- Receives messages submitted through the contact form on the portal sign-in page.
