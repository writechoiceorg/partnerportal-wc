## Access branding settings

1. Go to **Account & Settings** in the sidebar.
2. Open the **Portal Styles** section.

All branding and customization options are managed here.

---

## Customize colors

You can update background colors, text and link colors, button colors, and status indicators.

To change a color:

1. Enter a Hex or RGB color code in the input field, **or** click the color box to open the color picker and select a color.
2. A live preview of your changes will appear at the top of the page.
3. Click **Save** when done.

### How colors are applied

| Color | Used for |
|---|---|
| **Color 1 (Primary)** | Sidebar, sign-in page, and links |
| **Color 2** | Lead Phase: Consulting · Opportunity Phase: Interested · Payment Status: Scheduled |
| **Color 3** | Lead Phase: Prospecting |
| **Color 4** | Lead Phase: Lost · Opportunity Phase: Lost |

---

## Upload your logo

1. Scroll to the bottom of the **Portal Styles** page.
2. Click the **pencil icon** to edit and upload your logo.
3. Upload two versions of your logo: one over white, and one over your primary color.
4. Upload your favicon.

<Callout type="warning">
  All uploads must be in **.png format**.
</Callout>
