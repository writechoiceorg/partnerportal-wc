## Embed a video

1. Go to the **Announcements** tab in your portal.
2. Click **Insert** and select **Media**.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2172505520/f83c1257341c4f0c8a6483e07ce3/Screenshot%2B2024-06-26%2Bat%2B10_26_09-E2-80-AFAM.png?expires=1778093100&signature=b9c105299f6ab2e20a685fafcf094860c88e843e06a31158b4ef833a1737284e&req=diEgFMx%2BmIRdWfMW1HO4zVHXK6QXrFTOmcZxvnYltfSP4Hc4oNQquh0qBRJ7%0AA2N2KTMphF5w7o9sqxo%3D%0A)

3. In the Insert/Edit Media window, select the **Embed** option.
4. Paste your embed code into the provided field.
5. Click **Save**.
