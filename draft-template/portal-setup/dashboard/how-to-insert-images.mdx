<Callout type="warning">
  Images cannot be uploaded directly within the portal. They must be hosted on a third-party site first.
</Callout>

Images can be inserted into: a Resource description, an Announcement, an Opportunity, or Lead Comments.

---

## Step 1: Host the image

1. Upload your image to a third-party hosting site such as [imgbb](https://imgbb.com) or [Imgur](https://imgur.com/upload).
2. Once uploaded, right-click the image and select **Copy Image Address**.

---

## Step 2: Insert the image

1. In the portal, go to the area where you want to add the image (Announcement, Resource, Opportunity, or Lead Comments).
2. Click **Insert** and select **Image**.
3. Paste the copied image address into the **Source** field.
4. Adjust the image size as needed.
5. Enter any desired text and click **Save**.
