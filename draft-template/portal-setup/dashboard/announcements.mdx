Announcements allow Admins to share updates and news with partners. All announcements appear in the portal's dashboard.

Use announcements to share important program updates, highlight events or new features, or welcome new partners.

---

## Create and format announcements

The Announcement editor supports text formatting and the ability to add images, links, and media.

**Supported video platforms:** YouTube and Vimeo.

For step-by-step instructions on adding media:

- [How to embed a video](https://help.partnerportal.io/en/articles/6931959-how-to-embed-video-in-announcements)
- [How to insert an image](https://help.partnerportal.io/en/articles/7068232-how-to-insert-images)

---

## Send notifications

When saving an announcement, you can choose to enable **Send Notifications**.

| Setting | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Enabled** | An email is sent to all partners notifying them of the new announcement. |
| **Disabled** | The announcement appears in the portal only — no email is sent. |

---

## Pin and order announcements

You can control the order in which announcements appear on the dashboard.

1. Find the announcement you want to order.
2. Click the box next to the trash icon.
3. Enter the number representing the position you want it to appear — enter **1** for the announcement you want shown first.
4. Click **Save**.
