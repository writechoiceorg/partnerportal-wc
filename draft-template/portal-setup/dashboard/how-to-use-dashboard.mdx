When you log in to PartnerPortal.io, the first screen you see is your **Dashboard** — your central hub for monitoring activity and taking action within your portal.

<Callout type="info">
  This view is for **Admins only** and will look different from [what your partners see](https://youtu.be/SoaUgAijK4o?si=Av2Zx5MpEI2fB2Ou) in their portal experience.
</Callout>

---

## What's on the dashboard

**Portal Analytics** — shows how your partners have been performing over the past 30 days.

**Updates and Announcements** — the area where you share important updates with partners, such as program changes or new resources.

**Unprocessed Leads** — new leads that have been submitted and are awaiting action.

**Partner Applications** — new partner registrations that are pending your approval.

**Recent Payments** — payments recently issued to partners, including Pending and Completed statuses.

**Upcoming Payments** — payments scheduled to be sent to partners.

---

## Navigating from the dashboard

From the dashboard, you can quickly access all other sections and tabs within your portal.
