---
title: "How to delete a Resource"
description: "This article will walk you through the steps on how to delete a resource from the Resource Center."
---

1. **Locate the Resource**

  - Navigate to **Manage** under **Resources** on the sidebar menu.

  - Click on the category where your resource is listed.

2. **Select the Resource**

  - Find the resource you wish to delete.

  - Click on the resource to open its details.

3. **Delete the Resource**

  - Scroll down and find the **Delete** button.

      ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2386622980/161396d4ba45ccc40e71a5bb0e6d/image.png?expires=1779111900&amp;signature=17857a842a7cf50317ae2bb34d37257864bda38c7b98f056eefb69bb3f0d6e7a&amp;req=diMvEM98n4hXWfMW1HO4zVa6Boo%2F4qLCTTGWLIPTe9qdJt%2FUVtgp6RjbZy0e%0ATiU4%0A)

4. **Confirm Deletion**

  - A confirmation prompt will appear. Confirm that you want to delete the resource.

    - ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2386625576/9fddcb1bb2df2dd79298e6df5f3a/image.png?expires=1779111900&amp;signature=9c0fcd51b23737b0788e858892e8c23a89acbf86f005d166d463280bca6fdcc9&amp;req=diMvEM98mIRYX%2FMW1HO4zU%2BH23qz8bW3VTYdYia3ghg8aCOS4WfvzD3Pdy0u%0AYfnG%0A)

Once confirmed, the resource will be permanently removed from your Resource Center.

*For information on how to add a resource, check out [this article](https://help.partnerportal.io/en/articles/9015869-how-to-add-a-resource).*
