---
title: "How To Delete A Category"
description: "This article will walk you through the steps to delete a Resource Center Category."
---

Before you can delete a category, you need to ensure that no resources are assigned or associated with it. If your category contains resources, you have two options: **move the resources to another category** or **delete the resources**.

---

# Step 1: Remove Assigned Resources

### Option 1: Move Resources to Another Category

1. **Navigate to the Category:**
Go to the specific category that contains the resources you want to move.

2. **Select a Resource:**
Click on the resource you want to reassign.

3. **Edit the Resource:**
Click the **Edit Resource** button.

4. **Change the Category:**
In the editing screen, remove the current category and select a new category for the resource.

5. **Save Changes:**
Click **Save** to update the resource.

6. **Repeat:**
Repeat the steps for each resource in the category.

7. **Proceed to Delete:**
Once all resources have been moved out of the category, you’ll be able to delete the category.

---

### Option 2: Delete the Resources

1. **Navigate to the Category:**
Go to the specific category that contains the resources you want to delete.

2. **Select a Resource:**
Click on the resource you want to delete.

3. **Edit the Resource:**
Click the **Edit Resource** button.

4. **Delete the Resource:**
Click **Delete** and confirm your choice.

5. **Repeat:**
Repeat the steps for each resource in the category.

6. **Proceed to Delete:**
Once all resources have been deleted, you’ll be able to delete the category.

---

# Step 2: Delete the Category

Once the category is free of resources, follow these steps to delete it:

1. **Go to the Resource Center:**
Navigate to your Resource Center where all categories are listed.

2. **Open Category Details:**
Click on **Category Details** for the category you want to delete.

3. **Edit the Category:**
Select the **pencil icon** to enter edit mode.

4. **Delete the Category:**
Click the **trash icon** to initiate deletion.

5. **Confirm Deletion:**
Confirm the action when prompted to permanently delete the category.

---

By following these steps, you can ensure that your categories are managed properly and safely deleted when no longer needed. If you encounter any issues during the process, please consult our support team for further assistance at [support@partnerportal.io](mailto:support@partnerportal.io)
