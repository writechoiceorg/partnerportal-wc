# Lead

Leads contain important revenue and commission data. Some values sync from your CRM (if connected), while others are automatically calculated within the platform.

## Close Value

- Editable until the Lead is connected to a CRM.

  - Once connected, this value syncs from the CRM and becomes read-only in the portal.

- If not connecting to a CRM, this is can be edited manually in the portal once the lead has been approved.

NOTE: Close Value is for reference only. It is **not** used to calculate commissions.

## Total Revenue (Lead Level)

- The sum of all **Revenue** entries in the Lead’s *Revenue History*.

- Revenue can be:

  - Manually entered, or

  - Generated using the Payment Scheduler with your Accounting integration.

## Total Commission (Lead Level)

- The sum of **all** Commission Amounts in the Lead’s *Revenue History*.

- Represents the total commission paid to the Partner for ***that*** specific Lead.

# Commissions

As Leads generate revenue, Partners earn commissions. Each commission payout is recorded as a **Payment** in the Lead’s Transaction Log.

## Revenue (Payment Level)

- The revenue amount used to calculate **that** specific Payment.

- Can be:

  - Manually entered, or

  - Automatically calculated via Accounting integration based on the Payment’s Start and End dates.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2405675928/eeb36d15b2246e891fcadca2b939/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=d63c038ced2e0568e3e86046e4010bd7f2cf7200e6703487ebf16979f9c318de&amp;req=diQnE895mIhdUfMW1HO4zUODR0Th7Pw%2BgjEG5dMSOmVe%2FSHHmqPNuOt51AXP%0AOKMMWuDTHs%2Bix2WFMfs%3D%0A)

## Commission Types

Payments can be structured in several ways:

- **Fixed Amount** – A flat dollar amount (e.g., $50, $100).

- **Recurring Percentage** – A percentage paid on a recurring schedule (monthly, quarterly, annually).

- **One-Time Percentage** – A percentage paid once.

## Payment

- The actual commission paid to the Partner.

- Automatically calculated based on the Total Revenue and the commission structure.

# Partner

- The lifetime total revenue from all Leads submitted by that Partner.
