Announcements allow Admins to easily share updates and news with partners.

Use announcements to:

- Share important program updates

- Highlight events or new features

- Welcome new partners

**All** announcements are placed in the portal's dashboard.

## Create and Format Announcements

The Announcement editor allows you to:

- Add **images, links, and media**

- Format text using the built-in editor

**Supported video platforms:**

- YouTube

- Vimeo

For step-by-step instructions, see:

- [How to embed a video](https://help.partnerportal.io/en/articles/6931959-how-to-embed-video-in-announcements)

- [How to insert an image](https://help.partnerportal.io/en/articles/7068232-how-to-insert-images)

## Sending Notifications

When saving an announcement, you can choose to enable **Send Notifications**.

- When enabled, an email will be sent to **all** partners notifying them of the new announcement

- When disabled, the announcement will only appear in the portal

## Pin Announcements

After creating your announcements, you can select the order you'd like them to appear in your dashboard.

1. Find the respective announcement

2. Click on the box next to the trash icon

3. Enter the number you'd like the announcement to appear

  1. Enter **1** on the announcement you want pinned first.

4. Press *Save* when done.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2364782918/bfccc4dd24f9897fb48c0e8596f9/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=acb93b2f4c00e17976f225d08d0c1c6ed73273d80a158d6bc2e2d596bbcb0fcd&amp;req=diMhEs52n4heUfMW1HO4zYfAt6428pfy6GrlfNBOfOVw4H4QcBlcWHhawAQt%0AHLh4%0A)

You can also pin an already published announcement. To do so:

1. Go to **Manage** under the **Announcements** tab on the sidebar to see all the announcements.

  ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2364791066/0ec20ef8da1b000f66d9e5afc70a/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=7092bbe9cb91b3590f962a3f2b98055001f8c5a31d12c7ab8ff31ff040fa3dd0&amp;req=diMhEs53nIFZX%2FMW1HO4zYWymYBUV8Wyu7ucwyvAFouMENfpWpeH1wC7Aqsi%0AEsU4%0A)

2. Find the respective announcement and click on the **Pin** icon on the right.

  ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2364796458/1eae91a608efd1fadbabd4d42278/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=9e1e2fb0795572c2c09afc46b0005ffec053a9d4a90b0ffe669e3996eecd213e&amp;req=diMhEs53m4VaUfMW1HO4zTMJamg0CrxSldmga19cX9oZsM8zjSIrkbmR0amh%0Auud%2F%0A)

3. Check the **Pin this announcement to the top** checkbox and enter a rank number for the announcement.

  1. Enter **1** for the announcement you want pinned first.

4. Press *Save* when done.
