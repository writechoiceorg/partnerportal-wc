title: "How to Bulk Import Partner Users"
description: "This article will walk you through the steps on how to bulk import partner users to your portal."
---

# As An Admin

- Navigate to your **All** **Partners** section under **Partners** on the side menu and locate the partner for whom you'd like to import users.

- Scroll to the bottom of the page and find the **Partner Users** section.

- Click **Add Partner User** and select **Import CSV**.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388498552/2402fa8ffe98ae597338ba0f0839/image.png?expires=1779111900&amp;signature=45838fd2332cdfc0773699e080cdb95dc02e0a4196351cb74574b668b6b2d875&amp;req=diMvHs13lYRaW%2FMW1HO4zXI2O0bZgtteiKVnIV6HQ%2BMuwjMSV3dHWuq%2FmV6L%0AzAKF5WyKJMsvgxTwFVs%3D%0A)

- In the new window, download the template if you haven’t already. Once ready, click on **Select CSV file**, choose your file, and then click **Import Partner Users**.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388499954/75a6fa54a31896538c14692ce392/image.png?expires=1779111900&amp;signature=0a285b6a9f5da3dd4418c45e1b77e9b79b59f49eab850cf4f42e764b76547ba3&amp;req=diMvHs13lIhaXfMW1HO4zYEzQIoQcoopldkwPU1YiLJvTktDS6wNXVruqVfc%0AmsjCerUSRCtDsZPLmSM%3D%0A)

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388502205/e43de8590c629e95cb3b87a49896/image.png?expires=1779111900&amp;signature=c8da511b160a81d1c71df29c4599fae3a03bc0c83e79b31ff2cb2a96fc1fdd5a&amp;req=diMvHsx%2Bn4NfXPMW1HO4zUf%2F68OR%2FuVLnAvRT5YnPqnY7bpLtsk41V4pF1E%2F%0AGAwjcJQMDMezQvUdXEQ%3D%0A)

- If there are errors in the CSV file, an error message will indicate what needs fixing. Correct the errors and try uploading your CSV again.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388507956/e7a2ba83fc08910b3fa835309873/image.png?expires=1779111900&amp;signature=eafe994806a73da5b1e35082868528100551ee2e907f321e79cc0a16664ba133&amp;req=diMvHsx%2BmohaX%2FMW1HO4zR6J8wRVzfA4h6vtinJwZHWUqvs8tpIQ%2FQaxfQ90%0AcrX%2FXIsBgR4jqiYgpQs%3D%0A)

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388555614/674436ad514774a74ca52673b720/image.png?expires=1779111900&amp;signature=0ea6538032fd4ffb996fe84e58c6015d16088165d94a6e5e684850474edf1a26&amp;req=diMvHsx7mIdeXfMW1HO4zXYHmddz9k5z9sQR6%2BHUEHqrDSlVWqZfbCPmFpmz%0Ah%2F%2FeY%2FTgIRkHxlFG9nA%3D%0A)

- Once the import is successful, all users will receive an email with instructions to set up their account.

*For a visual walkthrough, check out our [step-by-step video](https://youtu.be/tIDcxbJurr0) guide.*

If the link in their welcome email expires, they can request a password reset with [these steps](https://help.partnerportal.io/en/articles/8694347-how-to-reset-password) to complete the setup.

# As A Partner

As a **Partner Owner**, you can also add your team members in bulk using this feature.

- Go to the **Team** tab under **Account**.

- Click **Add Team Member** and select **Import CSV**.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388570476/6b49a41a59eea15e3d1deebbc0bd/image.png?expires=1779111900&amp;signature=1acb476a7bfc88d0fca53f5f3e506a99111ae412f5bfe7fbf36fb0f993e83aa3&amp;req=diMvHsx5nYVYX%2FMW1HO4zdpT%2FOc%2BVXCdrIJyM2tu927TI71dkQRnDoacPSDz%0AUCzwTfuRSAMVkXU6Zy0%3D%0A)

- Download the template if you haven't already. When ready, select your CSV file.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388573253/9a0cc7c75505b350003cd33cd31f/image.png?expires=1779111900&amp;signature=fe1870be0480f523861ce3b5edccd89c74b3ad9a35ae7462c571611c80e70973&amp;req=diMvHsx5noNaWvMW1HO4zVahgUO8U%2BlrVLD4PNjJbZ4Yo3rLqKdqifs06fgW%0AQMaOQDoUz3CAtJ1Ti3k%3D%0A)

- Import your team members.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388575974/14a9b5ab82a2cfaafde42848269c/image.png?expires=1779111900&amp;signature=b570b8c109ace44177224309a346405f4bc2cd51ca1621ee7b379a51c397ff61&amp;req=diMvHsx5mIhYXfMW1HO4zVvHnALpTCinizRdN29MZsM9MBklVzb6CLs54OQT%0Aifgl8LcF3HLC%2F60A1wY%3D%0A)

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388576622/cfd857239a7282dfd70dea5d8881/image.png?expires=1779111900&amp;signature=cd3a3946949c7a6c319719c1f957514a01ca6fdcabd3c893cc4a96f8e081821f&amp;req=diMvHsx5m4ddW%2FMW1HO4zSwTVJtjaTsjUHZ0%2ByalLFfZ%2Bo1kmapJqdGMKf2l%0AVLrfAZBdPbsX8LuUpHs%3D%0A)

- If there are errors in the CSV, you’ll see a message detailing what needs fixing. Correct any issues and re-upload the file.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388577587/9dee7507fd3abd44050181f9ea54/image.png?expires=1779111900&amp;signature=f4cf565045e4d5a6b713056eab604768fba64e492fb2c8fd0f2114c2bb7373bf&amp;req=diMvHsx5moRXXvMW1HO4zU9ydbGJpdwrQ6yUb7s9c6nFTpxqWfTSg8F0DESM%0AuIcryAeAc9Yoc2QHXC4%3D%0A)

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388579410/5b70b41cbe03f8de174119c2ee75/image.png?expires=1779111900&amp;signature=5357f7519698abaf10a46a7fd238b95abc0eedf934def61f67270fb9fa240e48&amp;req=diMvHsx5lIVeWfMW1HO4zSWbwUAJh%2BG65xUmxbEWW6cEojDbRzN0nYOryT%2FN%0Afu9L1v%2FvRg6fVknUMN0%3D%0A)

- Once the import is successful, all users will receive an email with instructions to set up their account.

If the link in their welcome email expires, they can request a password reset with [these steps](https://help.partnerportal.io/en/articles/8694347-how-to-reset-password) to complete the setup.

If you have any questions, please reach out to our team at [support@partnerportal.io](mailto:support@partnerportal.io)
