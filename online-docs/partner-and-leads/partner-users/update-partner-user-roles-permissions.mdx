---
title: "How to Update Partner User Roles & Permissions as an Admin"
description: "This article will walk you through the steps on how to edit the roles & permissions for partner users as an admin"
---

As a Portal Admin or Portal Owner, you can update the roles & permissions for Partner Users (partner's team). Click on the **All Partners** tab under **Partners** in the sidebar and select the Partner Company.

Scroll down to the Partner Users section. Select the Edit button next to their name.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388486403/b6605fb195370802eb63c9eba532/image.png?expires=1779111900&amp;signature=92bee33a481dc889f5a9f034a0d1fa74d5b1c7e4e2f4100bcb65efbcfde5a2a4&amp;req=diMvHs12m4VfWvMW1HO4zaHvZ8XNaR02xNP2ITzfO3kn%2FkuD86ZS0AdgHBF%2B%0A8v4uBDY1AXYXe50WY%2Bk%3D%0A)

Once selected, a pop-up window will appear and show you the different permissions available. Select the desired one and press save.
