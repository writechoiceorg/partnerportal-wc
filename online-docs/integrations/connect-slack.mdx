**How to connect to Slack:**

Integrating your Slack account with PartnerPortal.io can be done in a few easy steps.

Log in to your portal and select the *Integration* tab found on the left-hand sidebar. If you don't see the Slack integration available, please ask our team to turn it on for you.

In the Integrations, find your Slack integration and select "**Enable Slack**".

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/834928906/4b7836ddafd1edec92095a22/screencapture-demo-partnerportal-io-integration-2023-09-20-09_20_16.png?expires=1778093100&amp;signature=a72ffd8b948b786e263d68f2d49f6d7a37b4ed9398665b93bf54e8dd39fadce4&amp;req=fCMjH8t2lIFZFb4f3HP0gFgL304NFpI9jVIbl3V4rzuGJyKuJ3hcXTy4MQOZ%0AsToJj4DGnXBQ6c0gKQ%3D%3D%0A)

Upon selecting, click the button on the next screen to officially "Enable Slack."

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/834929084/1bde03c6a7eb289c29534edc/screencapture-demo-partnerportal-io-integration-slack-details-2023-09-20-09_24_07.png?expires=1778093100&amp;signature=0763272d889393e984dc11c08b2e33c6f3d4025f6086879e78ffc16bcdb5111f&amp;req=fCMjH8t3nYlbFb4f3HP0gFTqAhUq2yhupNXjObMBDRkjaqk4zuVfZE7EW9W%2F%0AHZGtU9uNjaP6NWF%2BSg%3D%3D%0A)

From there you will be prompted to "Sign into your workspace."

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/834930942/f6ba2972d069e2070818c3c8/Screenshot+2023-09-19+at+3.52.22+PM.png?expires=1778093100&amp;signature=dd1e251f0d92f57e5850687c0688453f8c467ededb5fd09e5ccb5c881babdb19&amp;req=fCMjH8p%2BlIVdFb4f3HP0gOgHxQ7pR5wU8SuBCQQ%2BifYqNFHHrGbzFQhRumD%2B%0A3itpiIK%2Fb1eTUJWNJQ%3D%3D%0A)

After signing in, you'll be prompted to Allow the portal permission to access your Slack workspace. Select the "Allow" button.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/834927734/1fd8ff7b837c222b6e25e780/screencapture-partnerportaldemo-slack-oauth-2023-09-20-09_22_09.png?expires=1778093100&amp;signature=d4964866297f9ca2b3c4af0dc4be8121e3ccf39edfb15977c7d073be3dabf176&amp;req=fCMjH8t5moJbFb4f3HP0gNsa5AuyntGkj%2BviN8COeX8d3nL4dk8CiK4ZwlVh%0A%2BwxH9FRE8ToDa%2FGq%2Bw%3D%3D%0A)

Slack is now connected! You'll be redirected to Slack (app if downloaded) where you will now see the PartnerPortal.io connected app.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/834910672/cf76a8d4974ed6fe4b7139e3/Screenshot+2023-09-20+at+9.07.50+AM.png?expires=1778093100&amp;signature=3f4d25afd25b218f679c6965fb2f097f8590a69eb26d38c8908e904f194235b6&amp;req=fCMjH8h%2Bm4ZdFb4f3HP0gNQq7knermK2CFcFjE316N2vzg9CV8aYCNOUJVe9%0AAAcugL8U3fF9FjvQYw%3D%3D%0A)

You'll receive a few welcome messages with information on our Slackbot including an option to choose the channel you would like to post portal activity into.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/834911826/266524a9cec8d9e4522d3894/Screenshot+2023-09-19+at+3.55.29+PM.png?expires=1778093100&amp;signature=75decda60647b8a901631c53999647d4a7b893fabc30e773fb56eeb62b5f2f11&amp;req=fCMjH8h%2FlYNZFb4f3HP0gDD%2FFedGsmyso%2FnjCQjvVWKHxO1nfCW05UG2%2F382%0Av%2FqYk9ahlRv%2BekNY4g%3D%3D%0A)

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/834912973/08b03112515c93f47aeee487/Screenshot+2023-09-20+at+9.10.15+AM.png?expires=1778093100&amp;signature=3953e28fe8b3f71878d63f73742b1e1002d073af5624444e85b6bd26293e8255&amp;req=fCMjH8h8lIZcFb4f3HP0gH9dCXwgORJyBOf6k6S7ZuUV%2Bv%2F0yMLCfFCKV%2BMd%0AYYBOTWI5%2FvUne%2BCOgA%3D%3D%0A)
