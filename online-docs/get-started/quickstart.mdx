This guide walks you through the six steps to get your portal set up and ready for your first partner. Complete them in order — the whole process takes about 15 minutes.

## 1. Set your currency

> **Note:** This article is coming soon. In the meantime, contact [support@partnerportal.io](mailto:support@partnerportal.io) if you need help with this step.

Your program currency applies to all commission calculations and partner payouts. **This setting locks permanently after your first payment is processed**, so confirm your choice before going live.

---

## 2. Add your logo and customize colors

Before inviting partners, make your portal reflect your brand. You can set your primary color — used in the sidebar, sign-in page, and links — along with additional colors for lead and opportunity status indicators. A live preview updates as you make changes.

You'll also upload two versions of your logo (one on a white background, one on your primary color) and a favicon. All uploads must be in **.png format**.

[Customize your portal branding →](/portal-setup/dashboard/how-to-customize-branding)

---

## 3. Configure program settings and terms

**Program settings** let you describe how your partner program works, set your commission structure, and define partner tiers based on revenue performance. You can mark these details as public so prospective partners see them on your sign-in page before registering.

**Program terms** are optional. If your program requires partners to agree to a set of terms at registration, upload them under **Partner Program → Program Terms**. The same terms apply to all partners — they cannot be customized per partner. If you manage agreements outside the portal (e.g. via DocuSign), you can disable this section and attach signed documents directly to partner accounts instead.

[Set up program details and tiers →](/get-started/set-up-your-partner-program/how-to-setup-program-details)

[Add program terms →](/get-started/set-up-your-partner-program/how-to-add-program-terms)

---

## 4. Invite your team *(optional)*

If others on your team need access to the admin side of the portal, invite them from **Accounts & Settings → Team**. Only Portal Owners can add team members. You can assign each person a role to control their level of access and visibility.

[Add your team →](/portal-setup/team/how-to-add-team)

---

## 5. Connect a CRM or revenue tracking integration *(optional)*

Connecting your CRM lets you sync partner-referred leads directly into your pipeline and automate revenue tracking for commission calculations. PartnerPortal integrates with HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, and Zoho. For accounting and automated revenue import, you can connect QuickBooks.

[Connect HubSpot →](/integrations/hubspot/how-to-connect-hubspot)

[Connect Salesforce →](/integrations/salesforce/connect-salesforce)

[Connect Pipedrive →](/integrations/pipedrive/connect-pipedrive)

[Connect Zoho →](/integrations/zoho/connect-zoho)

[Connect QuickBooks →](/integrations/accounting/connect-quickbooks)

---

## 6. Invite your first partner

You're ready to bring partners on board. There are two ways to do it:

- **Self-registration**: Share your portal's registration link (`yourcompanyname.partnerportal.io/register`) and let partners sign up on their own. You'll receive a notification and must manually approve each request — automatic approval is not supported.
- **Direct invite**: Go to the **Partners** tab, click **Invite**, and add partners by email. You can also bulk-invite using a CSV file.

Only one person per partner company should register. Once they're in, they can invite the rest of their team themselves.

[Invite your first partner →](/get-started/set-up-your-partner-program/how-to-add-partner)
