If you need to reset your password, make sure pop-ups are **enabled** in your browser before starting.

These same steps can also be used if you were **invited to the portal but have not yet created your password**.

# Request a Password Reset Link

1. Go to your **portal login page**
​*(Example: `companyname.partnerportal.io`)*.

2. Enter your **email address** and click **Next**.

  ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388370605/6a91440b733aa04dd2cdedfebaee/image.png?expires=1782908100&amp;signature=9247aac18c6df0135e6ac3d977b8451ff91d3160239d28d70929ef1eaeabe898&amp;req=diMvHsp5nYdfXPMW1HO4zdVaF6PDnscH6lD4aCe%2BPUhjUiGgxMnv81RfQWMJ%0A4NEy%0A)

3. In the pop-up window, select **Reset Password**.

  ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388372600/0cb05cecc9952bd2c9b2cfa6e44d/image.png?expires=1782908100&amp;signature=fe10f0385343a369692bd74873f75367c02c820f3fa83b462446aff4b6480f7c&amp;req=diMvHsp5n4dfWfMW1HO4zRUgmvcWvzRCJcirEePgpfwAMAk1P6zO6E6CEmwz%0Am%2BJo%0A)

# If You Don’t Receive the Reset Email

If you do not receive the email within a few minutes:

1. Check your **Spam** or **Junk** folder.

2. If you still do not see the email, return to your **portal login page**.

3. Clear your browser’s **cookies and cache**.

4. Repeat the steps above to request the reset link again.

f you continue to experience issues resetting your password, please reach out to our team at *support@partnerportal.io,* and we’ll be happy to assist.
