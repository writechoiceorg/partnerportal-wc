- ## Free
  - Includes:

    - 2 Partners

    - 5 Leads

- ## Growth: $249 per month
  - Includes:

    - 50 Partners

    - 300 Leads

- ## Enterprise: $499 per month
  - Includes:

    - Unlimited Partners & Leads
