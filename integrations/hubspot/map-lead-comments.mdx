---
title: "How to map Lead comments to HubSpot?"
description: "This article will walk you through the steps to map your lead comments to your HubSpot deal."
---

(For steps on how to map custom HubSpot fields, please check out this [article](https://intercom.help/partnerportalio/en/articles/6977660-how-to-map-custom-fields-to-hubspot))

First, head to your Integrations tab and select your HubSpot settings. Click on the configuration button.

*(NOTE: only Admin accounts will have access to view the Integrations tab)*

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/669550266/6fd44f499b6e6ff5b817570a/Screenshot+2023-02-09+at+3.48.43+PM.png?expires=1778094000&amp;signature=23d55694e03372bdd994d3810cb7c7098c9c69cd63a32c70e2303f1cadc8f344&amp;req=ciYuE8x%2Bn4dZFb4f3HP0gFMsyLQsUHP2xLtHZmpt1pSjvTqfYidTn13oVufG%0Abk6BKGvtLsEjMfif3Q%3D%3D%0A)

You'll want to scroll to the Comment/Activity Tracking field at the bottom of your *Other Settings*. Select the button to the right to toggle the feature on.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/1301969249/6dded39a20ad5c523be1110a4b80/Screenshot+2024-12-17+at+4_16_16%E2%80%AFPM.png?expires=1778094000&amp;signature=a7ecb77d46666a9b181c70772243060f0457c9bf36a3b37362d1e97e45d247f3&amp;req=dSMnF8B4lINbUPMW1HO4zZSe%2BbkVmRfCa59XHS5InEcpSH3%2FGA4tqImQwDMA%0AZB7KLeR7dD0njsks%2BBQ%3D%0A)

---

Now, open any HubSpot deal mapped from PartnerPortal.io, and select *Filter Activity.* Select the PartnerPortal.io filter under Integrations. This will ensure you will have visibility on any activity.

*(NOTE: Each user will have to enable this filter)*

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/669557402/6dcb60909a4967677e0d903f/Screenshot+2023-02-09+at+3.51.50+PM.png?expires=1778094000&amp;signature=9e79c7ff09411c8b3995243d6112a48936d4e956d016a73e7285430f139fbcb4&amp;req=ciYuE8x5mYFdFb4f3HP0gAxd0i4qntAhVEONxxN0koOFpspE1kLE0mFZAq9j%0AERvu4AE42S%2FmkwuinQ%3D%3D%0A)

---

### PartnerPortal.io will only start to map comments as activity **after** the deal has been mapped to HubSpot. Any comment placed on the Lead record **before** being mapped, will NOT be mapped or visible.
