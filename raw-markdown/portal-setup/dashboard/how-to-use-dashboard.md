When you log in to PartnerPortal.io, the first screen you’ll see is your **Dashboard**. This is your central hub for monitoring activity and taking action within your portal.

# What You’ll See on the Dashboard

### Portal Analytics

See how your partners have been performing for the past 30 days.

---

### Updates and Announcements

Share important updates with your partners, such as program changes or new resources.

---

### Unprocessed Leads

View new leads that have been submitted and are awaiting action.

---

### Partner Applications

See new partner registrations that are pending your approval.

---

### Recent Payments

Track payments that have recently been issued to partners, including:

- Pending

- Completed

---

### Upcoming Payments

View payments that are scheduled to be sent to partners.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2172417655/ec9be5639963830264d662726972/Screenshot+2026-03-17+at+11_42_34%E2%80%AFAM+%282%29.png?expires=1778093100&amp;signature=3f3c3f6dbf3f8b20dc73be2ed92969f2f35ef207f75b487c5bce143295c75be9&amp;req=diEgFM1%2FmodaXPMW1HO4zeK6VLjvdRqTR%2B0PEDaR0AI1qjRMO0VcOcl7ZfZj%0A%2BhZDJexk69i1t8KATFc%3D%0A)

# Navigating from the Dashboard

From the dashboard, you can quickly access all other sections and tabs within your portal.

---

## Important Note

This view is for **Admins only** and will look different from [what your partners see](https://youtu.be/SoaUgAijK4o?si=Av2Zx5MpEI2fB2Ou)in their portal experience.
