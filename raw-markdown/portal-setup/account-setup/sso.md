PartnerPortal.io supports **Single Sign-On (SSO)** to simplify login and improve security. Our SSO is powered by **Auth0**, which allows you to connect a wide range of identity providers (IDPs).

---

## Supported Identity Providers

Because we use Auth0, you can integrate with **any identity provider supported by Auth0**, including:

- Okta

- Google Workspace

- Microsoft Azure AD

- LDAP

- And many others

**Note:** Okta is one of the most commonly used providers and is fully supported.

For a complete list of supported providers, refer to [Auth0’s Enterprise Identity Providers documentation](https://auth0.com/docs/authenticate/identity-providers/enterprise-identity-providers).

---

## Benefits of Using SSO

**Enhanced Security**

- Reduces password usage and lowers the risk of phishing attacks

**Improved User Experience**

- Users can log in once to access the portal—no need to manage multiple credentials

**Centralized User Management**

- Control user access directly through your identity provider
