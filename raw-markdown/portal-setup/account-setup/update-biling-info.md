# Update Billing Information

1. Go to **Account & Settings** in the sidebar.

2. Select the **Billing** tab.

From here, you can:

- View your **current plan details** (including billing cycle and account limits)

- Add or update your **credit card information**

- Enable **auto-pay** for future billing

# Update Company Information

### Subdomain (Your Portal URL)

Your subdomain is the default URL partners use to access your portal.

- Example:
If your subdomain is `companyname`, your portal URL will be:
`companyname.partnerportal.io`

**Important:**

- This is **not** a fully custom domain

- All standard portals include ***partnerportal.io*** in the URL

---

## Custom Domain (Optional)

If you prefer a fully branded experience, you can set up a **custom domain**.

- Example: `partners.youcompanyname.com`

**To set a custom domain:**

1. Enter your desired domain in the **Custom Domain** field.

2. Follow the instructions provided to complete setup.

**Note:**

- You must enter a **fully qualified domain** (e.g., `partners.yourcompanyname.com`)

---

## Support Email

You can also add a **support email address** for your partners.

- This email appears in all portal notification emails

- It will receive messages submitted through the contact form

The contact form is available on the portal sign-in page, allowing partners to easily reach out to your team.
