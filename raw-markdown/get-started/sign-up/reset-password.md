If you need to reset your password, make sure pop-ups are **enabled** in your browser before starting.

These same steps can also be used if you were **invited to the portal but have not yet created your password**.

# Request a Password Reset Link

1. Go to your **portal login page**
*(Example: `companyname.partnerportal.io`)*.

2. Enter your **email address** and click **Next**.

# If You Don’t Receive the Reset Email

If you do not receive the email within a few minutes:

1. Check your **Spam** or **Junk** folder.

2. If you still do not see the email, return to your **portal login page**.

3. Clear your browser’s **cookies and cache**.

4. Repeat the steps above to request the reset link again.

f you continue to experience issues resetting your password, please reach out to our team at *[support@partnerportal.io](mailto:support@partnerportal.io),* and we’ll be happy to assist.
