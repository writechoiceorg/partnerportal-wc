# Viewing Partner Engagement

Scroll to the bottom of your page and expand the **View History** tab to see all partners that have been assigned to the resource (via resource center categories)

For more information on how to set up your Resource Center, check out this [article](https://help.partnerportal.io/en/articles/6932138-setting-up-the-resource-center).

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2381870461/cdfd8d6ac15f12e20564d21af24d/image.png?expires=1779111900&amp;signature=db611617ad195ea6761585a6cf69e63b1a5fdd39260f2c6c50a9991f05286976&amp;req=diMvF8F5nYVZWPMW1HO4zX1oZSU6by4M%2FspVXb1YzsS4OhqVefF6Q4czPINK%0AfKcwBZ5mUC4XUtzRNSY%3D%0A)

Click **View History** on a specific partner to see which users at the partner company have viewed the Resource.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2381871982/03d8626821aa83bd21b121df414f/image.png?expires=1779111900&amp;signature=fef47a5b3a43475524479275a7423285fd641f439bb0d2483c70d1107529db12&amp;req=diMvF8F5nIhXW%2FMW1HO4zQzU3EBl6vjq7UYoWz9ORwhqbmQP5eXbYtQ%2FUw14%0AZgchjA%2BmmvQN7tCUsKQ%3D%0A)

Once a Partner has viewed the Resource, the history will update to reflect this.
