The Opportunities feature on PartnerPortal.io allows you to send leads and business opportunities to your partners directly within the portal. This feature streamlines collaboration and communication between you and your partners, making it easier to identify and pursue potential business prospects.

## Enabling Opportunities

To turn on the Opportunities feature, follow these steps:

- Navigate to **Settings** under **Opportunities** in the main menu.

- Enable the Opportunities feature.

Additionally, you'll need to determine the different *service types* that align with your business offerings. The "*service type*" is a dropdown menu that partners can easily understand, as it relates to their specific needs and interests.
