**Important Note Before You Begin:**

✋ You can **only** import leads for ***active*** partners in your portal. If you don't have any partners set up yet, you won't be able to import leads on their behalf.

# Importing a CSV:

Head to your **All Leads** tab under **Leads** on the sidebar. Click on the Register Lead button and select *Import CSV*.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388707479/aa93ac43bbffb337c6b9eaefb8dd/image.png?expires=1779111900&amp;signature=6ebdf3411e862f9964c680fd2ae48f6c1f79c720dc3027b41f9d2fbcad5203ec&amp;req=diMvHs5%2BmoVYUPMW1HO4zVBUd077iiUVR7pwsmbrba%2ByGuBnYme%2Fz9BK%2BYCK%0AtzbDlhcvPvcIM4X64rw%3D%0A)

*We only support uploading a CSV (**comma-separated values**) file.*

You can either download our CSV template or upload your own. Please note, that your your CSV must contain **all** the *required* fields of your Lead Registration form.

*For more information on how to customize your Lead Registration, please refer to this [article](https://help.partnerportal.io/en/articles/8732193-how-to-customize-the-lead-registration-form).*

After selecting your file, click "Import Lead." The system will upload and check for missing information for ***required*** fields, marking them as errors. *Optional* fields won't flag errors.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388707928/708e3d6181bf8566aa8a337f99d4/image.png?expires=1779111900&amp;signature=9af03014e079f9d917b7cf8ac05611c784e32b1fddafff47301a2758ccddd88f&amp;req=diMvHs5%2BmohdUfMW1HO4zapyud1Bxa46S3SYcQAeaWPlYNjDABoir50xT1a8%0AMHIRrmnta8Pift%2FWgBE%3D%0A)

If errors are found, the file will **not** import the Lead(s) until they have been corrected.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388709301/ad574cd0358fa6f7755650cf15b3/image.png?expires=1779111900&amp;signature=8c40b300c48f6ac08b861d384c0f938aa4696a7ccf2899bdc1908f423eb1944f&amp;req=diMvHs5%2BlIJfWPMW1HO4zd8Giz4p6WvqRWePp0KJuGHY1AU9E48kLyJPB74N%0AR%2B1MSE0zkgCGMIUlwZo%3D%0A)

NOTE: If importing multiple leads, each lead will be considered a separate row.

Please note that our bulk lead functionality will **not** search for any existing/submitted leads in your portal. We recommend confirming any existing leads ***before*** uploading your CSV file to avoid any duplicates.

# How to find the Partner and Partner User ID as an Admin:

When importing Leads, the Partner and Partner User IDs are **required** to ensure the correct Partner is associated with the lead.

Head to your **All** **Partners** tab and select the respective partner. You'll find their unique ID in their partner details.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388711951/6b0faa0e51902567604dbf71ad45/image.png?expires=1779111900&amp;signature=2f012919b990bef1ec6f73460ca44365aa6a29686bc2b80dd431747580f030ed&amp;req=diMvHs5%2FnIhaWPMW1HO4zUFl8%2FuH8YwherOuZsqk04pf8psLdoiq0u6EV%2FpI%0AU1Uo56RZs5MnnW4Mlcg%3D%0A)

To associate a deal with a specific Partner User on the Partner's team, head to the specific Partner and scroll to the bottom of the page to see their Partner Users. In the list of users, you'll see an ID section where you can copy the individual user's ID to associate them with the deal.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388714732/121736ce8fecd2688553ecfa71e6/image.png?expires=1779111900&amp;signature=67ec5003b9e75fab047da3f7dafafa63c7f9b5e7ad84ac37b8006b3004919399&amp;req=diMvHs5%2FmYZcW%2FMW1HO4zU7fSWlJDza5uSEkchTlRPB4cS6yvrIdJyPJ3qmI%0Am5hPVQVRYomNmzhpyzE%3D%0A)

You can also find the individual IDs by clicking the Pencil icon to edit the desired Partner User.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388716858/2965420e3a03992d0d6cd037f494/image.png?expires=1779111900&amp;signature=b7cb5b9254344e1e40d63a357db21f9c6821f96f9dad5bb7a0899e07b6834ce6&amp;req=diMvHs5%2Fm4laUfMW1HO4zTGkf7Prv1Fq9BpHDttlNxpqBrZ6LuXOjfvMD4Sp%0AvPM3d8695l5ezIsUQNw%3D%0A)

**Partner User ID is required to bulk import.**

# How to find the Partner ID as a Partner:

As a Partner, you can submit leads on behalf of your team. To find a Partner User's ID in the portal, head to your **Team** tab under **Account**. Their unique ID will be found next to their name under the ID section.

**NOTE**: If bulk importing leads for yourself, you will need to find your unique ID next to your name.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388721428/a0eb12eb094b876279a0bc8bd1a7/image.png?expires=1779111900&amp;signature=c7c0493592f12049c389c18068c616277dd7ca579d679686a10fd60d1789c61b&amp;req=diMvHs58nIVdUfMW1HO4zY2Sz9qsvj4AET6WzufbTN8UKrpDqC43zBB0b%2Fy1%0AHVFmIXEZo7YnMq4Xx7E%3D%0A)

# Successful Import:

Once you've collected all the necessary information and corrected any missing information, you'll need to import the file again. If no errors are found, you will see a message informing you of your successful import.

All leads imported will be placed in a *Pending* state and require approval from the admin team.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2388725146/7d5ca52709b5de7fa69a7a599d3a/image.png?expires=1779111900&amp;signature=51aa153644092be6ace982a32eebcd4afb96922809e3260e8aa9d63287d7e3cc&amp;req=diMvHs58mIBbX%2FMW1HO4zfoSfBgKyzAmUZody5fQnr9%2BKpwFHG3ZYpLkU88G%0Aw8LWSOQuToCROjpANEc%3D%0A)

Please ensure, you refresh your portal to see your new Lead(s).

*For more information on how to manage Leads, please refer to this [article](https://help.partnerportal.io/en/articles/6936987-how-to-manage-leads).*
