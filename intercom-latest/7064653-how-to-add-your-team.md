A **team member** is someone within your company or organization who needs access to manage or work within your portal. Only **Portal Owners** can add new team members.

# Adding a Team Member

1. Navigate to **Team** under **Portal Settings** on the sidebar.

2. Click **Add Team Member** in the upper-right corner.

  ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2364836026/571a1455377b7045a3450dd8586a/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=19cd8bb4ec394cd5cbb666c6e67e115f9298bc4c6c1d4a99afa3eeef075f1ac2&amp;req=diMhEsF9m4FdX%2FMW1HO4zWazW6UHgCTQcpk9WB1YEmfn5Xqw1rkBqwc5G7yQ%0A%2FjVB%0A)

3. Enter the required information for the new team member:

  1. First name

  2. Last Name

  3. Email Address

  4. Phone number

4. Click **Submit** to create their account.

**Note:** If you do not have a valid phone number for a staff member, any 11-digit number can be used.

## Managing Roles and Permissions

Each team member can be assigned a role with different levels of access.

To update a user’s permissions:

1. Click on the **Pencil** icon next to their name.

  ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2364842092/b7a16c3b35afd20f6abd4901bf20/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=4bae708fa4123faf1a01a9c4e8b9ea24d94e2ddf187efef75c27704dd6f7227c&amp;req=diMhEsF6n4FWW%2FMW1HO4zaPu9QBt51tpzEnZGmYXkYKToIT35I4%2BtfY6tVH9%0AkHhi%0A)

2. Select the appropriate role in the pop-up window.

  ![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2364849464/df5be8297ded100121d65bc4151d/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=0b9fed406e756130ca7400e6a39b52f8fa31a774594d131fbbb6321733ef83f6&amp;req=diMhEsF6lIVZXfMW1HO4zUjUruWnUYTnzX%2FtKuY4JDmF08Ei96eXCCn6UUsx%0AzwhK%0A)
