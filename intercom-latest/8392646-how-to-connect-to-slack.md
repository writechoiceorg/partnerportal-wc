**How to connect to Slack:**

Integrating your Slack account with PartnerPortal.io can be done in a few easy steps.

Log in to your portal and select the  **Integration** tab found on the left-hand sidebar. If you don't see the Slack integration available, please ask our team to turn it on for you.

In the Integrations, find your Slack integration and select "**Enable Slack**".

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2374381740/4cb080bd4daff2d5130b0e575e75/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=340c232ec6db2fd88cbc8dc2b2b4d18c95f157e252d0a3be7fdb6d4c17619d95&amp;req=diMgEsp2nIZbWfMW1HO4zVeqx3JYblR7w8AQIUu3rVFqdfGI8yDBJwHRbUcx%0ALdi483aqanErhtWcQbE%3D%0A)

Upon selecting, click the button on the next screen to officially "Enable Slack."

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/j01nt7mc/2374382301/3a5c0d36b53d9d361d04c2879cf8/image.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=158b2e252a0f72aad3cc7bdf0107c1d1081b333e8dbaccf62e4bc3ea12c99f24&amp;req=diMgEsp2n4JfWPMW1HO4zUEtY6gbEha41ubWDbKYXTT4Bmrndsl6b3nMjfsF%0AjB7squeWGnABw%2B01fK4%3D%0A)

From there you will be prompted to "Sign into your workspace."

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/834930942/f6ba2972d069e2070818c3c8/Screenshot+2023-09-19+at+3.52.22+PM.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=f68ae9d38e6afe10f917d29c08e1a5c8acb04b576380d42535909fd949b8d6eb&amp;req=fCMjH8p%2BlIVdFb4f3HP0gOgIzg7hQpwU8SuBCQQ%2BifayMLm%2B%2Fq5sRhsNhDXo%0ACphKYvgmK9sqo3M7yQ%3D%3D%0A)

After signing in, you'll be prompted to Allow the portal permission to access your Slack workspace. Select the "Allow" button.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/834927734/1fd8ff7b837c222b6e25e780/screencapture-partnerportaldemo-slack-oauth-2023-09-20-09_22_09.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=7cb86d60e4ea783c913b79ba1436a8963e2fc1a3e45aeb31c88617d7cfb76ad6&amp;req=fCMjH8t5moJbFb4f3HP0gNsV7wu6m9Gkj%2BviN8COeX%2F2U1uZCDyUUuGwBtYG%0A7h7J%2BlM%2FFxrYRhT15w%3D%3D%0A)

Slack is now connected! You'll be redirected to Slack (app if downloaded) where you will now see the PartnerPortal.io connected app.

![](https://downloads.intercomcdn.com/i/o/834910672/cf76a8d4974ed6fe4b7139e3/Screenshot+2023-09-20+at+9.07.50+AM.png?expires=1783016100&amp;signature=337b00b7dd0c8d1d6568177dce4c1c5c7012e90de3f802d33199cb50a70e3596&amp;req=fCMjH8h%2Bm4ZdFb4f3HP0gNQl5UnWq2K2CFcFjE316N0WdRD%2FrqJBTxgUs65W%0A1GA6HfIscXv91e%2BJYg%3D%3D%0A)
